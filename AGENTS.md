# BPD research-agent instructions

Before conducting or interpreting a benchmark in this repository, read the
[EvalMath mechanistic-resolution skill](https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/EvalMath/tree/main/skills/evalmath-mechinterp-power)
and run its `scripts/preflight.py` on independent development-unit effects
and matched sham variation.

A useful mechanistic experiment declares an observable *causal* outcome,
physically matched intervention dose, mechanistic minimum effect, independent
unit, controls, multiplicity family, selection exposure and power before GPU
training or a broad readout sweep. Individual tokens, Sudoku cells, loop
iterations, alternative perturbation angles and two checkpoints reusing one
board are repeated measurements rather than new independent roots.

For BPD and LOOPSCOPE use the correct-cell recovery/corruption accounting,
full-head parity, state-radius and actual displacement checks in addition to
future-logit capture. A donor that harms fewer cells than a random control
does not thereby repair clean reasoning. Observational probes and exact
linear-algebraic BPD witnesses belong to different evidence classes from
native model-causal claims.
