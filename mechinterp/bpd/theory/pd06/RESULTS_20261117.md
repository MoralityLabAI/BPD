The count-supervised arm's mean inferred count accuracy was **75.4%** on unseen pairs and **95.5%** on the unseen triple, while exact support was 31.2% and 56.2%. Cardinality recovery therefore often coexisted with wrong **which-component** decisions.

Even an oracle that supplied the **true count** while leaving assignment and the dictionary trainable recovered zero of 32 seeds. The learned read axes of that arm still matched the true rotated basis in 29/32 seeds. The oracle-support arm supplied the entire true latent support mask during training and evaluation, and recovered all 32 seeds with negligible reconstruction error. This sharply separates basis representability from learned support assignment.

The count-supervised planted-frozen positive control succeeded in only 6/32 seeds, which demonstrates that even granting the true dictionary does not reliably solve the novel support combinations under the fixed MLP gate and optimizer.

### Reconstruction-only control

The dense all-component arm obtained nearly zero output reconstruction MSE across every family while scoring **zero exact support recovery**. It independently confirms that matching the physical identity map is insufficient evidence that a recovered dictionary has the correct causal component activity.

## Population L0 mathematics and proof scope

The latent source selects k=1,2,3 uniformly, then selects a support subset from its frozen k-specific training family. For independent continuous active amplitudes and an invertible orthogonal rotation Q,

\[
z(Qe_j)=\Pr[j\notin T],\qquad
(z_0,z_1,z_2,z_3)=\frac{1}{36}(19,15,19,19).
\]

The sum equals 2, and idealized expected L0 of the exact coordinate read basis is **4−2 = 2 active factors per pass**. Because every singleton support has positive mass, every mixed latent read direction has strictly lower hyperplane zero mass than its constituent coordinate reads. Linear-matroid basis exchange makes the rotated coordinate reads uniquely optimal up to scaling and permutation.

[RotatedSupportMass.lean](RotatedSupportMass.lean) kernel-verifies the finite weights, pairwise strict dominance on the source supports, and held-out support separation. The full real-valued probability, arbitrary mixed-read and basis-exchange theorem remains a paper derivation; the quantitative neural PD06 observations do not certify a Lean theorem or global minimum of the finite-step straight-through optimizer.

## Interpretation and next falsifier

The study provides evidence of a **compositional generalization failure of learned factor gates** under unknown rotated four-dimensional observations. The fully trained count-supervised arm learns a nearly exact rotated read dictionary and frequently infers the correct cardinality, yet fails to select the correct components on support combinations absent from fitting. Direct support supervision improves this transfer, and a true-support oracle completely resolves the original physical reconstruction problem.

All seven-arm comparisons and t intervals outside the frozen primary gate remain secondary or posthoc. The experiment has **no cross-rotation zero-shot transfer test**: each seed trains and evaluates under its own fixed rotation. Generalizing to a different unseen orthogonal basis without adaptation would be a separate, stronger hypothesis.

Further work should distinguish a missed support-selection rule from optimizer-specific memorization by testing a gate constrained to use learned factor coefficients directly, adding a dictionary-equivariant thresholding policy, or conducting explicit controlled novel-support interventions. Each direction requires a new source-locked seed cohort. Existing PD01–PD05 results remain unchanged.
