# BPD-SF05-A2: finite nonlinear public-level-set repair on excluded roots

**2026-11-18, post-Stage-A development-only result.** The [hosted A2 workflow](https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/BPD/actions/runs/44137074611) verified the separately frozen [A2_LOCK.json](A2_LOCK.json), passed 17 unit tests and ran the two preexisting, excluded SF04 teachers 901/902. Raw receipt: [stage_a2_excluded_hosted.json](results/stage_a2_excluded_hosted.json) and [GitHub Actions artifact](https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/BPD/actions/runs/44137074611/artifacts/14351948704). The earlier [Stage A nonlinear failure](RESULTS_STAGE_A.md) and its source-lock are unchanged. This *post-result* method is explicitly not an independent prospective scientific confirmation.

## Observed numerical outcome

The original full physical source-tag donor write was close to the public role's finite readout level set. Correcting its **actual** residual with the privileged public-query Newton oracle produced a public-output difference of approximately 1–2 × 10^-5, while **retaining 100% authorized donor retrieval** on every eligible pair. All candidate arms converged to the frozen absolute public tolerance 10^-4, including the shams, and all maintained exactly the same current scalar readout.

| Excluded root | Eligible authorized | Full physical BPD authorized recovery | Public error before | Public error after | Mean correction/original dose | Singleton 14-factor recovery after repair | Tangent-then-finite full recovery |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 901 | 242 | 100.0% | 0.01115 | 0.0000117 | 0.71% | 50.4% | 27.7% |
| 902 | 263 | 100.0% | 0.01598 | 0.0000197 | 1.55% | 74.5% | 38.8% |

These two root cohorts contain **505 authorized eligible pairs**. All full physical BPD, teacher-C physical and exact hidden-donor repaired edits retain 100% donor retrieval. Output-SVD rank-16 BPD edits also retain 100%. The independently trained physical factor reconstruction MSEs were `1.909244e-7` and `4.845523e-8`.

The original Euclidean public-tangent projection removed roughly 15% of physical write norm and caused large finite public-output changes. Starting Newton correction from that tangent-projected write restored public equality but produced only 27.7% / 38.8% authorized recovery. The finite level set is therefore an important constraint on *where* to correct an edit, not simply a property of the tangent plane at the recipient origin.

## Matched nonprivileged and privileged controls

The identical A2 solver was applied to 16 random same-initial-L2-dose current-dark interventions and 16 **pre-correction signed-authorized-gradient matched** interventions for each of three candidate arms. For the full physical BPD arm:

| Excluded root | Repaired BPD full recovery | Repaired random-dark recovery mean | Repaired authorized-gradient-matched recovery mean | Full BPD correction/original dose | Signed null correction/original dose |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 901 | 100% | 10.1% | 6.4% | 0.71% | 19.56% |
| 902 | 100% | 9.5% | 13.9% | 1.55% | 22.43% |

**Comparison limitation:** Signed-gradient matching is imposed on the *initial* shim; applying the nonlinear correction generally changes its final authorized gradient effect. The null is thus a privileged **pre-correction** gradient match, rather than a complete final-matched causal null. The matched shams tend to require much larger relative corrections and may be less aligned with a reachable donor trajectory.

## Evidence boundary

The solver observes and differentiates the **future public model output** repeatedly, then optimizes each edited state until that output returns to baseline. Exact public preservation is therefore partly guaranteed by how the privileged solver is constructed. It establishes a feasible solution for the tested trained synthetic states, not a deployable secrecy rule or evidence of discovering a canonical BPD mask. The teacher's public readout is a single scalar, with source identity represented as a structured one-hot input.

All root outcomes are previously used development roots. There is no fresh-root population CI, no 59-root tolerance calibration, no source-family-disjoint replication, no real model tokens and no empirical leakage finding. These results do not update SF04's registered 3/8 partial-routing failure or SF03's 0/32 strong-effect failure.

**Next prospective design:** study a learned *nonprivileged* correction policy trained on separate source histories, with source-family-disjoint heldout roots. Compare against the exact A2 oracle, unchanged physical write, original tangent projection, source-blind prediction, full physical teacher, and re-corrected random-sham arms. Freeze the 95/95 calibration population and utility/public tolerances before generating new roots. Keep ARG-03-N's sealed 168 cases untouched.
