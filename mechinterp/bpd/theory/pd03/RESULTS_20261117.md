## Posthoc mechanism analysis, distinctly labeled

**Top-1 routing:** All 24 trainable dictionaries learned the planted read axes (minimum axis cosine 0.9960); every one of the 3,072 held-out four-pass trajectories selected exactly the required one-hot mask. The model's exact-binary gate fraction was 0.5 by construction. The observed mean MSE was 1.3273e-5. This supports reliable *execution selection under a one-required-component assumption* on the registered 2D source.

**Margin calibration:** Threshold selection used only the 128-example calibration set per seed. It chose 0.45 in 21 seeds and 0.50 in three seeds. The calibrated arms had **no required false-offs**, but they produced 2,048 extra activations of the incorrect component, concentrated in the five failed seeds 277,293,307,317,397. Their route fraction was exactly zero on each failed seed because a redundant component remained active on one or both phases. All five retained binary MSE <=0.02. Extra gates are thus invisible to reconstruction-only success. The failed seeds had 256,512,512,256,512 false-ons respectively.

**Fixed-threshold hard baseline:** Training the original PD-02-style independent threshold at 0.5 on fresh seeds yielded 3/24 recovered models, with 951 missing required activations and 858 extra activations. Its failure rate varies with the initialization cohort, consistent with the original PD-02 negative result. These results are a new registered arm, not a re-run or correction of PD-02.

**Controls:** A top-1 constraint on mixed-source inputs, where both coordinates are needed on every pass, produced a mean held-out MSE of **0.48818** and mean gate fraction 0.5, with 0/24 achieving MSE <=0.02. Calibrated margin gating on mixed-source inputs learned to keep nearly all gates on (mean density **0.99959**), with mean held-out MSE **0.01996** and 22/24 meeting MSE <=0.02. Both SVD-frozen positive controls reached exact 24/24 phase-routing and zero binary MSE. The top-1 architecture's task-specific capacity limit is therefore directly measured.

## Scientific interpretation and boundaries

The intervention is a hard model capacity constraint: at most one rank-one contribution can execute for a given token/pass. On these phase-disjoint inputs, that capacity happens to match the planted activity exactly. It blocks the PD-03 margin arm's redundant double-activation solutions and the previous PD-02's missing required component failures. A source requiring two simultaneous independent contributions exceeds this capacity, as shown by the mixed-source negative control.

The registered source family has two components, four passes, and independently sampled sparse coordinates. A fully general neural decomposition may have overlapping supports, multiple simultaneous mechanisms, adversarially chosen states, and non-linear latent interactions. These conditions were absent. The results establish a narrow controlled optimizer finding, with no general real-network mechanism-identification claim.

The extra gate census and error localization were calculated **after** the prospective data run and are descriptive. They did not determine seeds, hyperparameters, thresholds, the full-seed recovery definition, or the original scientific pass/fail decision.

**Next candidate:** test adaptive cardinality where both single-component and multi-component execution are required in different samples, with a new distribution-locked mixed and nested-support source. Keep top-1 as a control rather than assuming it generalizes.
