# BPD-PD-10: cold-start smoothing and the mixed-pair attractor

**First registered run, 17 November 2026. Preregistered scientific verdict: FAIL. Engineering verdict: PASS.**

PD-10 prospectively compared an unlabelled orthogonal sparse-read optimizer initialized at a narrow Gaussian smoothing width \(\sigma=0.15\) with the original wide-to-narrow PD-09 schedule \(0.6\to0.3\to0.15\). Twenty-four fresh model seed/rotation pairs and six frozen arms completed. The cold arm succeeded in **2/24** complete six-family mechanisms, versus **0/24** for original annealing. Its two paired wins gave exact McNemar **p=0.5**, below the registered success requirement.

## Mechanistic failure localization

The original annealing schedule again converged to the familiar geometry: two nearly exact source read directions and a mixed near-45° pair, with mean minimum component cosine **0.71864** over 24 seeds. It produced low binary output MSE while delivering **zero exact complete four-pass support** on every OOD pair, triple and transition family.

The cold 0.15 arm escaped into a four-axis solution in only two seeds. Most other random initializations ended with mixed read directions, including cases of more than one mixed pair; its mean read cosine was **0.78284** and its mean minimum matched cosine was **0.65411**. Its higher mean MSE compared with original annealing reflects more severely mixed random-start solutions. Cold 0.30 and warm 0.30→0.15 still frequently converged near one mixed pair.

**Privileged 42° stress control:** the fifth arm deliberately used the true source rotation Q to initialize coordinates (0,1) as a 42° mixture, while optimizing the same source-only smooth mass at sigma 0.15. This diagnostic arm failed in **all 24** seeds and finished at mean read cosine **0.85826**, close to the two-exact-plus-two-45° geometry. It provides direct optimizer evidence for a narrow-bandwidth local attractor near 45° in this controlled initialization family. Because it used Q at initialization, it is **explicitly privileged and excluded from unsupervised recovery claims**.

The [PD-09 restricted quadrature](../pd09/landscape_audit.py) showed that coordinate reads have greater total near-zero mass than 45° reads at sigma 0.15, while 45° remains a local maximum. The new 42° stress test is consistent with that restricted energy landscape and explains why reducing sigma alone does not reliably remove the trap. The random-start cold results also show that beginning below the crossover is insufficient to ensure convergence.

The privileged count-supervised arm recovered the true read directions and complete binary support masks in **24/24** new rotations, confirming the physical source and fixed read-threshold evaluator can solve this synthetic task.

## Mathematical and causal boundary

PD-10 trains a differentiable orthogonal QR factorization using only observed dense x in its four unlabelled arms; it does **not** train an unconstrained original BPD physical factor parameterization during that gradient phase. It installs the resulting orthogonal read/write factors into the true BPD wrapper for intervention evaluation. The active source amplitudes are bounded away from zero, the hidden mixer Q is orthogonal and the target physical map is linear identity. These are strong restrictions.

The population expected-L0 objective has the rotated coordinate basis as strict optimum under the registered PD06 support law. The Gaussian smooth surrogate has local mixed-pair structure for all tested widths. The registered failure does not contradict exact population identifiability. A future unlabelled method needs a mechanism to distinguish **true structural zeros from near-cancellation of co-active factors**, or to escape persistent pair-rotation extrema, with fresh seeds and a new frozen protocol.

Earlier PD01–PD09 first-run receipts remain unchanged. No real recurrent network or 7M Sudoku TRM mechanism recovery is claimed.
