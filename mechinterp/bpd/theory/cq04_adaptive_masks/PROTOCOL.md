# BPD-CQ-04 prospective protocol: input-adaptive component masks on learned recurrence

Frozen design date 2026-11-17. This is a **synthetic developmental/pilot** study. The seed-to-seed teacher/factor fit is the independent root. The planned eight roots cannot calibrate a 95th-percentile/95%-confidence nonparametric sham bound under the EvalMath mechanistic-resolution preflight, which requires at least 59 *independent calibration roots*. Even a successful eight-root run must be described as a pilot, not confirmatory mechanism evidence.

## Research question and reuse

Test whether learned, per-input hard BPD masks preserve independently defined **two-pass behavioral targets** at a lower execution-component budget than a pre-calibrated input-blind mask mixture. Preserve the native `BPDModel/TiedComponentLinear` implementation, with `delta_mask=0`. Train independent physical and BPD factor stages exactly as in merged BPD-CQ-03, with 520 Adam physical updates and 360 Adam factor updates, two rank-two shared factors and learned rather than prescribed recurrence. Both physical factors and both BPD factors are trainable in their respective stages and frozen before controller training. The CQ-04 controller is trained afterward, with BPD V,U frozen; hence the study tests **mask learning over learned fixed components**, not joint BPD-gate coadaptation. Inference masks are exact binary values.

## Data isolation and independent labels

Engineering seeds 997,1009,1013,1021 are excluded from the prospective eight-root cohort. Frozen pilot seeds: **701,709,719,727,733,739,743,751**. For each root, teacher train seed offsets are inherited from CQ-03. Distinct RNG streams (seed offsets +432000, +433000, +444000, +447000) supply 4,096 controller-training examples, 2,048 input-blind-control calibration examples, 256 evaluation examples and 128 donor transfer pairs, respectively. Generation makes 40% dual-source cases with independent `a,b ~ U[-.8,.8]`, 30% source-A-only (b=0) and 30% source-B-only (a=0), via a prespecified categorical RNG. Task targets are **(a,b)**, independent of V,U and mask selection. No prior ARG-03-N seal, private test, other experimental receipt or pretrained model is opened.

## Controller and selection exposure

For development-only target-guided imitation, enumerate all 16 binary two-pass/two-component masks, each with four execution slots. For each input choose the smallest cardinality plan producing both readouts within **.12 absolute error**; break same-cardinality ties by smaller max error, then by fixed lexicographic pattern order. If no plan achieves the target, choose smallest maximum error (the failure remains in the training labels). This label enumeration uses *only controller-training examples*. A two-hidden-layer tanh MLP, width 48, maps `(a,b,|a|,|b|)` to four mask logits; train with BCE, Adam lr .006, 800 updates, minibatch 256 using a fixed seeded generator. At evaluation threshold the four logits at zero to obtain hard 0/1 masks. The gate sees initial state features at both passes: it is **input-conditioned two-pass planning**, not feedback control from later hidden states. Source-family modes are not provided to the gate.

On independent calibration examples choose the best mixture of at most two **constant** four-bit masks (all 16 patterns) to maximize two-pass binary success subject to expected active-slot cost <= the gate's calibration mean. Enumerate all pairs and their budget-feasible mixing weights; freeze the choice before evaluation. This is the primary competing null, stronger than a generic random mask at equal budget. Further controls: full four-slot native execution ceiling, and independent uniformly permuted slot positions at each evaluation case's exact gate cardinality. The static mixture is sampled with RNG +445000, permutation control +446000. The full mixed-source (dual) stratum is reported separately.

## Registered readout and causal controls

Primary per-root contrast is **adaptive minus calibration-optimal static mixture**, scored on all 256 evaluation cases at the .12 criterion for *both* future readouts. Report each root's hit counts, mean actual slots (0..4), all three source strata, full and matched-random-slot arms. Scientific minimum of interest is **+0.15 success probability per independent root at equal mean slot budget**, with at least 6/8 roots positive and a full-task success floor of 0.90 per root. This is an exploratory pilot threshold. Evaluate paired outcomes by root and report all outcomes; do not divide 2,048 cases into 2,048 independent roots. Two comparisons (static mixture, permuted slots) form the declared family; report multiplicity-aware direction/sign evidence and refrain from calling a confirmed positive in this eight-root design.

For donor transfer, create fresh paired dual-source inputs sharing a, with recipient b in [-.2,.2] and donor b shifted by signed magnitude in [.30,.45]. Evaluate independent donor-b output. Eligibility requires recipient and donor masked clean success, equal four-bit plans and a nontrivial component write. Post-first-pass recipient patch is `((h_d-h_r)@V * recipient_mask_pass1) @ U`. Keep the recipient pass-two execution mask fixed for all arms. Compare the native BPD component write, its current-dark projected and norm-matched version, a random current-dark vector of the same **per-pair physical norm**, a sham, and a larger-budget full intermediate-state donor replacement. Report eligible denominator, immediate visible disturbances and dose error; never use donor target to choose patch, gate, or mask. Transfer success uses the fixed .12 absolute donor-b tolerance. This is diagnostic alongside the primary two-pass outcome.

## Geometry and native execution

Check exact full-operator `GL(2)` shear invariance under `R=[[1,1],[0,1]]` and quantify fixed-mask behavioral drift under the shear, retaining the originally trained execution-mask indices. Gauge-dependent singleton components remain noncanonical. The independent CPU backend implements masked read/write multiplication, while the repository-native backend **must** execute `BPDModel/TiedComponentLinear` through `PassMaskSchedule` for each two-visit trajectory and single-visit donor continuation. Compare native and independent per-arm counts exactly; actual native numerical parity tolerance <=1e-5.

## Pre-data power and interpretation

The independent unit is a teacher/factor seed including its source world, 8 pilot roots, 256 scored cases/root, 128 donor pairs/root. A conservative practical variation floor is 0.15; sham-tail floor is **uncalibrated** until 59 independent calibration worlds are collected, so the strict EvalMath preflight returns `REDESIGN_OR_PILOT_ONLY`. Control selection sees its own calibration set; controller selection sees its imitation training set. There are no heldout-dependent hyperparameter changes, seed replacement, threshold changes or additional scored arms after this source/protocol is frozen. Retain negative results, mixed-source failures, source hashes, and a source-locked outcome receipt.

A future independent confirmation must use a new frozen seed roster with >=59 separate development/sham calibration worlds, at least eight independent pilot effect worlds, an explicit root-level power estimate and a separately sealed confirmation cohort. A positive CQ-04 pilot would motivate nonlinear/real TRM follow-up, not certify that capability.
