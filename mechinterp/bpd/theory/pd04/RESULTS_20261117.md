On this 2D source, the **ideal true cardinality** is 1 for disjoint, 2 for overlap, and 1.5 on average for nested. The learned adaptive count implied respective mean cardinalities **1.418, 1.862, 1.542**. That population-level agreement on nested data masks many pass-local mistakes: extra gates in single-support passes and missing writes in overlapping passes.

The adaptive frozen-dictionary arm has exactly correct read axes (cosine 1.0) and still fails all 24 seeds. The oracle cardinality arm, using the true count but training component selection and factors, succeeds 22/24 seeds, attaining perfect overlap and nested support agreement across the cohort. Its two failures, seeds 463 and 521, arise from reconstruction MSE in the disjoint family, so the count oracle does not trivialize the entire task.

### Distinguishing decomposition from low reconstruction

The calibrated independent comparator reconstructed well on **all three families** at a mean MSE of 0.00037. Its 6/24 full support-recovery score falls because it often keeps redundant mechanisms active on disjoint and nested passes. The fixed all-two arm similarly reconstructs nearly perfectly with **zero complete disjoint/nested support recovery**, providing a direct negative control for reconstruction-based identification.

Top-1 has mean overlap MSE **0.48014** and nested MSE **0.24193**. The fixed one-factor execution budget is inadequate when both independent components must write. These controls confirm the cardinality intervention is meaningful while exposing limits of each training rule.

## Mathematical connection to idealized expected L0

For the registered balanced mixture and pass-uniform sampling, the exact continuous source law has

\[
z(e_1)=\Pr[x_1=0]=\frac16,\qquad
z(e_2)=\Pr[x_2=0]=\frac13.
\]

Any projective read direction with both coordinates nonzero has zero hyperplane mass: on single-coordinate inputs its read never cancels, and on the independent continuous overlap there is zero probability of exact cancellation. The two coordinate directions are therefore the unique maximum-hyperplane-mass read basis, up to scaling and permutation. The exact idealized expected-L0 optimum is

\[
J_{\min}=2-\left(\frac16+\frac13\right)=\frac32
\]

active components per pass, or mean gate fraction **0.75**. The oracle-count arm reaches this structural activity level by construction.

This is an analytic statement for the registered source law, independent of training. The **learned** models optimize a surrogate with discrete forward gates and straight-through gradients. The prospective failure does not contradict idealized strict uniqueness: the dictionary can be identifiable while optimization fails to learn correct state-dependent cardinality.

## Remaining questions and experiment boundary

The active problem is the gating objective and its dynamics. The learned count head receives nonzero gradients, yet it often settles on a nearly uniform propensity for both factors, producing asymmetric false-on and false-off errors. A separate diagnostic should determine whether the issue comes from saturated count decisions, weak incentive to remove redundant writes, or the particular straight-through gradient. A controlled cardinality supervision experiment may be informative, but it changes the information available to the gate.

**No new hypothesis was inserted after seeing held-out outcomes.** The original PD-04 negative result and all prior BPD-PD-01/02/03 receipts remain unchanged. A future run requires new seeds and source registration. The findings concern this exact 2D shared-weight toy and make no claim of trained mechanism identification in the 7M Sudoku TRM.
