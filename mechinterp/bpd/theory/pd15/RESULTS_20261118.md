## Runtime and scope

Mean single-thread search times were 5.868 seconds (uniform_wide), 6.033 seconds (weighted_wide), 5.877 seconds (uniform_narrow), and 6.042 seconds (weighted_narrow). The fixed lambda=1 weighted reduction increased measured time by approximately 2.8% relative to matched wide unweighted search on this CPU implementation. Comparing absolute Gaussian training masses across different lambda values is not an appropriate recovery metric.

The explicitly designed strong-margin source has correlated anchored-support motifs with probability 0.85, magnitudes uniform in [0.75,1.25], and a hidden orthogonal rotation. The observed norm serves as a proxy for low-cardinality examples under this source law. **This is a validated source-specific identification result on a sparse-linear identity teacher.** It is not a theorem of global optimum uniqueness for arbitrary Gaussian-smoothed sparse sources and does not establish discovery of learned mechanisms in nonlinear TRM/transformer weights.

A next preregistered transfer test should freeze this weighted optimizer and test genuinely new source distribution families, including shifts in activation cardinality, cluster probability, magnitude and dimension. Separate physical interventions in a trained recurrent model will be required to support mechanistic claims outside this synthetic teacher. All PD-01 through PD-14 source code, gates and first-run scientific outcomes remain intact.
