### Why one seed failed

**Seed 1237** did not learn the source-aligned dictionary under the frozen count-supervised stage-one budget: mean read-axis cosine was **0.79375**, below the preregistered ≥0.90 requirement. It had poor exact support even with read-refit (for example unseen pair 0%, withheld triple 3.125%). The remaining 23 stage-one dictionaries met the read-axis criterion. This exception was preserved and was not retrained, discarded, or replaced.

The model-seed/rotation pair is the statistical unit. Individual passes and support-mask cells provide diagnostics but are not treated as independent replicates in the p-value or Wilson intervals.

## Causal read/write correspondence and ablation

The shuffled-read refit had access to the **same pretrained dictionary**, the same read magnitudes, the same training budgets and threshold parameterization as read-refit. It cyclically shifted the read features relative to their physical output component labels, and recovered **0/24** seed mechanisms, with zero exact four-pass support on every evaluated family. This is a controlled perturbation of the read-to-write routing correspondence.

The zero-fit read gate recovered **23/24**, matching the fully refitted primary. Hence the main transfer result follows from having recovered factors with sufficient read-coordinate alignment; additional gate-only optimization primarily reduced mean binary output MSE (from 0.001059 to 0.000468) while leaving the seed-level mechanism score unchanged. Refitted read thresholds averaged 0.7095, while the zero-fit thresholds remained 0.5.

The original count-supervised top-k gate on the **same factor dictionary** achieved withheld-pair routing 27.47%, withheld-triple routing 45.23% and withheld-transition routing 35.03%, consistent with PD-06's failure to generalize raw-input assignment rules. The raw-independent gate also generalized poorly after identical gate-only fitting on the same dictionary.

## Scientific interpretation and limits

The data support a **conditional mechanistic transfer theorem in a specific synthetic source class**: once count-assisted factor learning aligns a read dictionary close enough to an orthogonal sparse basis, individual component activity can be inferred by thresholding the component's own normalized read coefficient, without retraining on new support combinations. The exact population and finite quantized threshold arguments are in [PD07 theory](../pd07/READGATE_THEORY.md) and [ReadGateSeparation.lean](../pd07/ReadGateSeparation.lean).

The stage-one supervision provided true cardinality (k=1,2,3) for each training pass. These labels are privileged information, and the result **does not** show unsupervised factor discovery. The target is the identity physical map on rotated four-dimensional sparse data, with amplitudes bounded away from zero and fixed Q per seed. General recurrent transformer states may have nonlinear mixing, overlapping nonlinear modules, weak amplitude gaps or changing read bases. The result supports a transferable routing construction under its stated assumptions, not general neural causal identification, not a converged exact-L0 optimizer, and not a pretrained 7M Sudoku mechanism.

All earlier PD-01–PD-07 source-locked original results remain intact. The next prospective test should remove the count-supervised dictionary step or replace it with a verifiable non-oracle self-supervised alignment mechanism, while keeping the frozen read-gate transfer and rotated held-out support audit.
