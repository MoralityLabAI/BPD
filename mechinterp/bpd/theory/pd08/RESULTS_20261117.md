# BPD-PD-08 | Registered two-stage frozen-dictionary gate transfer

**17 November 2026. Preregistered scientific verdict: PASS. Engineering verdict: PASS.**

Twenty-four fresh seed–rotation pairs completed the source-locked study. Each seed trained one physical BPD read/write dictionary with the PD-06 count-supervised top-k objective, then copied that exact dictionary into five gate/control arms with factors frozen bit-for-bit. The label-free read-conditioned gate recovered **23/24 full mechanisms** across six seen and unseen support families; a raw-input independent binary gate recovered **0/24** on the same learned dictionaries.

## Factor discovery versus gate fitting

The mean read-axis cosine across all 24 first-stage dictionaries was **0.98956**, with a distinctly bimodal distribution. **Twenty-three** learned dictionaries had every individual aligned read cosine greater than **0.999805**, exceeding the source-specific conservative sufficient threshold **0.99333** derived in [PD-07 READGATE_THEORY](../pd07/READGATE_THEORY.md).

On those 23 well-aligned dictionaries, the **fixed read threshold 0.5** produced **exact masks on every held-out board across all six families**, including unseen pairs and triples, without any stage-two gate training. It made zero false-on or false-off events. This matches the analytic margin prediction and shows that basis recovery, rather than fitting the threshold, explained the successful transfer.

The trained read threshold also recovered 23/24 seeds. Its mean threshold drifted to **0.73425**, approaching the allowed upper bound of 0.75 and the minimum active input magnitude of 0.75. Across the 23 successful seeds, the trained threshold accumulated **77 false-off events and zero false-ons**. It preserved the registered per-seed acceptance gate while producing less exact component execution than the fixed 0.5 read threshold. This secondary comparison was observed after the preregistered test and is not a new primary positive claim.

The raw-input MLP reached **72.89%** complete singleton support on average, then fell to **27.34%** on withheld pairs, **3.43%** on the withheld triple, and **18.49%** on the unseen-support transition. Reconstruction loss sometimes remained small even when component selection failed.

## Seed 1237 is the factor-discovery failure

Seed **1237** was the only unsuccessful seed for trained read, fixed read and the full-support oracle on the learned dictionary. Its stage-one mean read-axis cosine was **0.74986**, minimum matched cosine **0.60201**, and four aligned cosines were approximately [0.74608, 0.99953, 0.65181, 0.60201]. Even *perfect* source support masks on its learned dictionary yielded mean reconstruction MSE **0.11239**, revealing a physical factorization failure.

The planted frozen-Q control for seed 1237 succeeded with exact support and numerical-zero MSE. No seed was dropped, retrained, retuned or replaced after seeing this exception.

## Scientific limits and continuation

This is evidence that **componentwise read-gating transfers compositionally once a sufficiently accurate factorization has been learned**, for the registered linear sparse-latent 4D source and identity teacher. The trained raw-input MLP with the same frozen physical factors was unable to meet the strict six-family support gate under the matched stage-two budget.

Stage one used **true cardinality labels** to aid dictionary discovery. Stage two was label-free, but the overall procedure is a *partly supervised pipeline*, and this PASS does **not** establish fully unsupervised discovery of hidden mechanisms. The source has orthogonal linear mixing and a minimum active-amplitude margin. General nonlinear latent encoders, recurrent interactions, cross-rotation zero-shot transfer and 7M Sudoku TRM mechanisms remain outside the evidence.

The next falsifier is to retain the fixed read gate while **removing count supervision from stage-one factor discovery**, using a separately preregistered unsupervised sparse-dictionary objective with fresh seeds and the same combinatorial holdouts. Earlier PD-01 through PD-07 original reports and outcomes remain unchanged.
