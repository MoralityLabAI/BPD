## Exact sparse execution and the search basin

The globally searched read dictionary reached **100% exact four-pass support** in all six held-out families: seen singletons, seen pairs, unseen diagonal pairs, seen triples, unseen triple, and transition using withheld combinations. It had **zero false-on and zero false-off component activations** throughout these families. Its minimum individual read-axis cosine among all 24 seeds was **0.999559**, exceeding the conservative 0.99333 sufficient execution-margin threshold derived in PD-07.

Each unlabelled arm used the **same initial random QR factor basis** and **the same 1024 training boards** per seed, giving 4096 observed four-dimensional vectors. The same six ordered column pairs were searched over six sweeps, with **73 angles evaluated at each pair step, 36×73=2628 candidate evaluations per seed**. Both used the Gaussian near-zero-mass score at fixed σ=0.15. The global angular range was −90° to +90° in 2.5° steps; the local range was −5° to +5° with the same candidate-evaluation count.

Global search required **5–11 actual nonzero rotations per seed** and reached near-perfect read alignment in all 24 fresh rotations. Local angular updates often improved the smooth objective and reconstructed reasonably while retaining the wrong latent component selection. Its mean withheld-pair exact support was 4.58%, and its unseen triple 6.62%.

The **privileged true-Q stress controls** deliberately started with a 44° mixture of two source axes. One global angular move escaped the mixed-pair attraction per seed and yielded 24/24 complete support recovery. The local version remained trapped in all 24 seeds. Because those stress initializations used the true hidden source Q, they are separate diagnostic controls, excluded from the unlabelled primary comparison.

## Scientific scope and next falsifier

Primary and local training objectives accessed **only observed dense x and orthogonal read factors**. True latent support and count labels did not enter their optimization. Source Q was used to generate training samples and later to evaluate alignment; only the separately named privileged pairmix arm used Q to initialize model factors. All final interventions passed through the actual BPDModel/TiedComponentLinear shared physical identity teacher, with residual writes disabled and exact binary masks.

This result supports **unlabelled source-factor recovery in a fixed four-dimensional orthogonal synthetic class** using a finite, nonlocal pair-angle search. It establishes a route around the 45° smooth-loss local optimum encountered in PD-09/PD-10. Its fixed angular grid, pairwise search and bounded-amplitude latent law are substantial inductive biases. Runtime and candidate-evaluation cost can grow with factor dimension, source complexity and required angular resolution.

The registered study does not prove a generally convergent expected-L0 optimizer or identify recurrent neural mechanisms in the 7M Sudoku TRM. The next prospective test should vary latent dimension, source correlations and minimum active amplitude, keeping unseen support combinations and matched candidate score budgets. Previous PD-01 through PD-10 source-locked outcomes remain unchanged.
