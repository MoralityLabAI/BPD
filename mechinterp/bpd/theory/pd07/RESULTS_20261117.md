## Error localization

The trainable primary arm recovered mean read cosine above the registered 0.9 threshold in only **4/24 seeds** (population mean 0.8050). Its mean read threshold settled near 0.4122, ranging from approximately 0.311 to 0.599. Its average executed gate density 0.6601 exceeded the true held-out density 0.5417. The global factor representation often remained mixed despite good task reconstruction.

Its aggregated gate errors are:

These are nested per-pass/component events, not independent model replicates. The primary inferential unit remains one independently initialized seed–rotation pair.

The read-detached variant also recovered just 1/24 seeds, with mean read cosine 0.8105. This provides no evidence that the feature-to-factor gradient solves the dictionary problem under the registered budget. The count-supervised raw-input control recovered read cosine >=0.9 in 21/24 seeds but selected exact components on withheld pairs only 22.22% and on the withheld triple 36.39%. Its result recapitulates PD-06's compositional assignment obstacle on fresh rotations.

## Mathematical interpretation and next falsifier

When read columns equal planted Q columns up to scaling, signs and permutation, normalized read coefficients are precisely the latent activity magnitudes, and any threshold in (0,0.75) recovers all support subsets. This sufficiency theorem says little about how joint training finds that basis. The trained read-coupled and detached arms reached suboptimal representations; their gates therefore failed on seen as well as unseen support mixtures.

The strongest next control is a **two-stage factor-discovery experiment**: first train the dictionary with the count-supervised PD-06 objective on training-only data; then freeze V,U and fit the componentwise read gate with no further labels, comparing against an equally trained raw-input independent gate on the same frozen dictionary. The first stage should use fresh seeds; the second stage must preserve calibration and held-out separation. This explicitly tests whether the read-gate inductive bias transfers once factor discovery is solved.

The present result concerns a four-dimensional synthetic identity-teacher task and no pretrained TRM. Earlier PD-01 through PD-06 original receipts and registered pass/fail outcomes remain unchanged.
