## Native BPD component effects and matched costs

All four arms used identical fresh original 2048-board source pools per seed, the same planted hidden Q and original source seed, the same initial orthogonal QR basis for learned arms, and the exact PD12 physical BPDModel/TiedComponentLinear four-pass identity-teacher evaluation. The three learned arms each evaluated nine sweeps × 120 lexicographic read pairs ×37 global-angle candidates, or 39,960 angle scores and 327,352,320 candidate-by-observed-vector scores per seed. The norm_aligned and norm_shuffled arms used the same 8192 positive weights up to a seed-only random permutation.

| Exact full-four-pass withheld quartet support | **100%** | 0% | 0% |
| Mean effective training fraction | **0.482630** | **0.482630** | 100% |
| False-off gate entries in original eight-family census | **0** | 31,829 | 31,829 |
| False-on gate entries in original eight-family census | **0** | 83,925 | 83,925 |

Norm aligned and the privileged planted-Q oracle each achieved exact component support on every heldout board in each of the eight source families, with no false-on and no false-off gates. Uniform and shuffled models reconstructed the easy identity teacher at low MSE, but repeatedly routed the source-specific components incorrectly.

The observed advantage therefore depends on the **association between each observation and its norm weight**, rather than the marginal distribution of weights or the effective sample-size reduction alone. This is a stronger source-specific causal attribution of the *optimizer's training signal* than PD-15's first weighted-versus-unweighted result.

## Scope and next study

The source is the same strongly clustered 16-dimensional, orthogonally rotated synthetic sparse latent identity teacher as PD-15. It has 25% singleton-support examples and positive amplitudes bounded in [.75,1.25]. The results do not establish that norm weighting works when input energy is independent of active-cardinality, or that a learned recurrent nonlinear model's physical writes have the same observable geometry.

[PD-17](https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/BPD/pull/79) prospectively tests that radial/cardinality assumption using paired original and radial-camouflaged laws, a separate observed-only angular identification baseline, the same physical mechanism endpoints and a 32-root paired interaction gate. PD-17 and its future scientific receipt are independent of this published PD-16 first run.

These findings are distinct from BPD-NC-04's unconstrained recurrent fidelity limit, the nonconservative native dynamics in LOOPSCOPE-08, and EvalMath's independent-model-root variation floors. A true nonlinear recurrent causal mechanism claim will require separate physical pass-indexed mediation tests.
