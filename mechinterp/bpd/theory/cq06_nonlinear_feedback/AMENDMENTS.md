The prospective protocol was originally pushed as BPD commit `070b4f6` before any of its new 59 sham-calibration or 26 confirmation roots were opened. All exploratory changes in this note were made exclusively on the *excluded development seeds* before the new cohorts were scored.

- Gaussian random-dark vectors were insufficiently stringent in engineering tests. An unrelated matched-parity **clean first-step donor direction**, independently sampled from a root-specific source, transfers substantially more often. The two independent physically equal-dose matched-manifold directions are now the primary null and the sham-calibration pair; Gaussian nulls remain recorded as diagnostic arms.
- In eight excluded development roots the BPD-minus-manifold effect was 0.07 to 0.30, with mean about 0.228; the original preflight planning target 0.40 was revised to 0.30. The 15-point scientific minimum, primary two-component singular mask, 26 confirmation roots and all original model training hyperparameters remain frozen.
- The 59 root calibration cohort, which trains fully independent rotated recurrent worlds, supplies both matched-manifold sham-envelope samples and development paired-effect variance for the EvalMath skill. Independent confirmation is still kept completely untouched until the preflight gate.

This record is a disclosure of the pre-test adaptation, not evidence of prospective confirmation. All 59 sham and 26 confirmation root effects must be preserved even if the registered threshold fails.
