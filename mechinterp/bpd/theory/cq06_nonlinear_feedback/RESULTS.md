# BPD-CQ-06: registered synthetic confirmation under delayed nonlinear recurrence

**Completed 2026-11-18 (Asia/Manila).** Evidence class: registered confirmation **within the frozen synthetic rotated-parity/tanh recurrent family**, subject to the off-manifold caveat below. The original protocol was committed at `070b4f6` and the matched-source-null amendment was committed before any sham or confirmation roots were scored. Independent model roots: 59 sham calibration and 26 separately withheld confirmation. The source hashes, paired outcomes, strong controls, exact tests and failure seeds are immutable. This is not a demonstration of natural-language transformer mechanisms.

## Prospective preflight and evidence population

- EvalMath strict preflight: **READY_FOR_CONFIRMATION**; 59 independent calibration worlds; the 59th-ranked worst sham difference is **10.156 pp** and the registered scientific minimum is **15.0 pp**. Hence the effective threshold is **15.0 pp**. This is the sample-maximum upper tolerance bound for a population 95th percentile at 95% confidence assuming independent worlds.
- The independent randomization unit is a newly rotated generator world, newly trained physical tanh recurrence, its separate BPD rank-four factorization and feedback policy. Each confirmation root supplies 256 paired-z task cases and 128 donor pairs. The **26** independent root units support inference; 6,656 task rows and 3,328 donor pairs are repeated within-root measurements.
- The preflight used a **30 pp planning target**, and observed the calibration cohort mean primary effect **15.850 pp** (SD **13.184 pp**). Its normal planning approximation estimated **8** roots for 80% nominal power, less than the 26 withheld confirmation roots. The true development mean was near the 15-point practical threshold; the confirmation data were not consulted to adjust the design.

## Task: delayed new evidence and adaptive execution

The generator rotates first-visit cues (x,y) into a private four-dimensional code and provides a new z cue only at visit two. Labels are sign(xy), then sign(xz). Paired z/−z cases have identical first-visit input and opposite final labels, giving a provable 50% exact myopic first-cue-only ceiling. The learned physical shared rank-four core and state transition use tanh, and a learned factorization is frozen before the gate learns.

## Severe geometric limitation and competing mechanism

The label “matched-manifold null” describes the **source direction**. Its final projected and norm-matched patched state is often outside the reachable tanh box and is **not** itself an on-manifold intervention. At confirmation, **3,143/3,328 (94.44%)** current-dark BPD patches and **2,979/3,328 (89.51%)** matched clean-source null #1 patches leave the tanh coordinate range [-1,1]. Both are evaluated by continuing the recurrent system from these artificial states; their effect could involve the dynamics off the natural recurrent-state distribution. Unprojected BPD and full donor replacements remain within bounds, while Gaussian random directions nearly always leave. The registered tests establish a causal difference between **artificial interventions** in the frozen synthetic model; they do not identify naturally reachable causal fibres or prove the mechanism in real TRMs. See [GEOMETRY_AUDIT.md](GEOMETRY_AUDIT.md).

### Registered verdict

**PASS for both prespecified causal contrasts and model-cost gate within this precise nonlinear rotated-parity generator**, conditional on the recorded off-manifold intervention limitation. Generalization to natural hidden-state causal mechanisms, nonlinear geometry on reachable states, SAE latent identification, 7M TRMs and actual language models is an open question. A prospective in-range tangent/geodesic control is the highest-priority next test.
