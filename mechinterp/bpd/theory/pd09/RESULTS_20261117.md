# BPD-PD-09: registered unsupervised hyperplane-mass basis experiment

**First registered run: 17 November 2026. Scientific verdict: FAIL. Engineering verdict: PASS.**

This source-locked study evaluated whether unlabeled annealing of a Gaussian near-zero activation penalty recovers four orthogonal latent read directions from dense observed inputs. Each read factor was evaluated with the same fixed binary magnitude threshold on six seen and withheld support families. The 24 independently seeded rotated models produced **0/24** full mechanism recoveries in both annealed and fixed-bandwidth primary arms. A separately privileged true-count-supervised positive control succeeded **24/24**.

## Posthoc 45° mixed-pair attractor

Inspecting the original frozen read-cosine receipt, the annealed orthogonal objective consistently learned **two near-perfect coordinate axes** and **two rotated/mixed axes**. This pattern occurred in **24/24 model seeds**. Each of these mixed axes had absolute matched cosine about 0.71–0.73, whereas the true coordinate axes had cosine approximately 1. The mixed pair was latent indices (1,2) in 13 seeds and (0,1) in 11. The mean minimum matched cosine was **0.71735**, far below the registered ≥0.9 mean-axis gate, and below the stronger per-component support-margin condition.

The [posthoc deterministic pair-rotation landscape audit](landscape_audit.py) computes Gaussian near-zero kernel mass by integrating the *original training support law* with 128-point Gauss–Legendre quadrature. The calculation is restricted to a one-pair rotation submanifold with the other two axes held fixed. Under the common support weights 36, the exact coordinate pair has sharp zero masses summing to 34/36; two 45-degree reads have exact sharp zero masses summing to 18/36. In the limit σ→0, the coordinate reads are better by **16/36**.

At positive smoothing widths, the Gaussian kernel counts *near* cancellations of simultaneously active signed coordinates. In both tested representative pairs, the rotated 45° dictionary minus the exact coordinate pair has **smoothed near-zero mass**:

| Bandwidth σ | 45° minus coordinate mass, two reads | Favored in the restricted comparison |
|---|---:|---|
| 0.60 | **+0.043988** | 45° mixed pair |
| 0.30 | **−0.109854** | coordinate pair |
| 0.15 | **−0.228449** | coordinate pair |

The equality crossing occurs at approximately **σ=0.430**. Crucially, the 45° mixture remains a **local maximum of smoothed near-zero mass** at all three widths. The registered schedule started at σ=0.6, where the mixed-pair solution is favored. Reducing sigma improves the *global* coordinate comparison while leaving the mixed-pair local attractor available to gradient descent. This supports a concrete initialization/path-dependence explanation for the stable 24/24 mixed-pair result.

This numerical landscape is a **posthoc diagnostic**, separate from the preregistered success test. It is a restricted two-read rotation analysis, not a proof of the full four-dimensional optimization landscape or a global convergence theorem. Original result and hyperparameters remain unchanged.

## Interpretation and next falsifier

The original exact expected-\(L_0\) population theorem predicts a unique rotated coordinate read basis for this source, with two active components per pass. The finite-sigma Gaussian approximation has a different landscape; its gradient ascent toward near-zero mass can reward signed *coactivation cancellations* that do not represent persistent latent atom inactivity. The true-count-supervised control recovered all four physical read axes in 24/24 seeds, confirming the frozen evaluation pipeline can recover mechanisms given privileged count information.

The next prospective test is **unlabelled cold-start small-bandwidth optimization** on independent rotations. A σ=0.15-from-step-zero arm should be compared with the original annealed schedule and a cold-start σ=0.30 arm under matched training budgets, plus an explicit 45° mixed-pair initialization stress control. That test must freeze source IDs, seeds, optimizer budgets and success thresholds before any new model training. A success would remain a 4D synthetic source-specific result, with no automatic claim about pretrained recurrent transformer mechanistic interpretation.
