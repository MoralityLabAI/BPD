These checks exclude the interpretation that the phase-disjoint trained result was a silent fixed-dictionary gate fit.

**Where the failure sits:** Seed 71's trained disjoint gate means were approximately (0.859,0.830) and (0.857,0.829) on phase-0 passes, then (0.852,0.832) and (0.852,0.832) on phase-1 passes. The SVD-frozen controls route phases successfully in both seeds. They demonstrate that the objective/gate family can recover phase routing given planted factor alignment, without establishing that training the dictionary recovers it reliably.

## Frozen-stage switch penalty

Seed 71's trained-disjoint stage 2 selected coefficient 0.05 on calibration. Its calibration switch fell **0.02632 → 0.01439**, while gate level increased **0.83752 → 0.84245**, just within the registered +0.005 tolerance. This is the anticipated failure mode of switch regularization: it can reduce transitions by keeping gates active through gaps. Seed 89's trained-disjoint selected coefficient 0 and retained its phase-specific routing. No stage-2 update changed either dictionary.

The mixed-source negative arms display low MSE with near-constant, high-density gates. The seed-89 mixed dictionary also aligns with the axes (0.9884), so axis match alone is insufficient evidence of phase-disjoint sparse execution.

## Interpretation and next proof/control boundary

This experiment trains a **surrogate** with continuous sigmoid gates, mean gate sparsity, and a physical faithfulness term. It therefore cannot falsify the mathematical theorem for exact expected L0 over invertible dictionaries. The result does show a concrete local optimizer failure to select the strict sparse decomposition despite a tiny reconstruction loss. A claim of BPD mechanism recovery requires a distinct stronger learned-dictionary test, with explicit exact-/hard-mask behavior and a broader independent seed cohort.

The companion [FiniteFiltration.lean](FiniteFiltration.lean) currently kernel-proves the general finite rank-profile optimality equivalence **conditional on a simultaneously rank-saturating greedy witness**. Proving that witness's existence from the vector-matroid independence axioms remains a separate formalization task. New trained controls, whether positive or negative, do not discharge that proof obligation or alter the original B1–B4 gates.

**Stopping record:** Freeze this result as a failure. Any changes to the sparsity loss, optimizer, initialization, train budget, phase generator, or recovery threshold belong in a separately registered experiment and must report this failed first run alongside it.
