# CQ-05: state-feedback mask causality and canonical gauge

Let H be a learned rank-two physical linear transition with a BPD factorization Hᵀ=VU. A binary component mask m at pass t gives F_m(h)=((hV)⊙m)U. CQ-05 learns two policies π₁(h₀) and π₂(h₁). The trajectory is h₁=F_{π₁(h₀)}(h₀), h₂=F_{π₂(h₁)}(h₁). It is adaptive to the state actually reached after the first pass, including intervention states. A two-pass mask plan from h₀ alone cannot change its second decision after a late intermediate-state patch.

Independent labels are \`a,b\` from h₀=(0,0,a,b), so correct behavior requires |h₁[0]−a|≤ε and |h₂[0]−b|≤ε. The first policy is trained using a supervised exhaustive 16-plan oracle on *development examples*. The second is trained using an exhaustive four-mask oracle on states reached by its frozen first policy. Oracle labels are never consulted at test. Both controllers can exploit task-specific synthetic structure, especially the first policy's direct access to a,b; the support-aware deterministic calibration baseline quantifies this shortcut.

The donor patch after pass one is δ=((h_d−h_r)V⊙π₁(h_r))U, using the recipient's first-pass plan. A current-dark control projects δ into the kernel of q(h)=h[0] and restores its physical Euclidean dose to ||δ||; a random kernel vector is matched pair-by-pair. The future donor target b_d is generated independently. Both π₂(h₁+δ) and π₂(h₁) are tested against b_d. The intent-to-treat denominator includes pairs that change plans, fail clean-task eligibility, or have zero dose.

For any R∈GL(2), \`(VR)(R⁻¹U)=VU\` while a singleton component mask generally fails \`D_m R=R D_m\`. Choosing \`(V*,U*)\` by the singular value decomposition of the **complete full operator VU** yields a canonical rank-one component basis when the two nonzero singular values are distinct. Equivalently reparameterized V and U yield the same canonical singular directions, except sign flips that cancel within each read/write pair. Singular-value degeneracy leaves a rotational ambiguity; finite-precision near-degeneracy can perturb canonical masked behavior. CQ-05 measures this explicitly, using double-precision SVD with fixed bounded shears and retaining the original hard gate in the raw-gauge control.

An eligible-only donor statistic is \`Pr(success | clean, equal plan, nonzero dose)\`. CQ-05's primary estimand is the unconditional \`Pr(success)\` over the full preregistered source population. A difference in those estimands measures intervention-selection bias; the former does not determine the latter.

The root is a complete independently trained W/BPD/controller triple. Multiple test examples and interventions on a root are correlated. A Monte Carlo sign test across eight roots and a two-comparison Holm correction may describe directionality, but an uncalibrated sham-tail floor and task-family reuse prohibit general causal-interpretability confirmation under EvalMath.
