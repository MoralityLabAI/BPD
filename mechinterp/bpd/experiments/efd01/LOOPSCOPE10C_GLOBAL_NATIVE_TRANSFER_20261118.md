## Registered physical geometry

Their clean task targets are right-cyclic shifts of source strings; the donor's predecessor input digit changes at one prescribed site. The current full digit head is linear and applies the same rank-ten 10×48 physical matrix at all 81 tokens. Thus the all-token head Jacobian has rank 810. `K = ker(D head_all_tokens) ∩ ker(dE_shared)`

## What drives the effect

The registered additional energy-gradient constraint gives almost **no additional binary task selectivity** beyond ordinary current-head invisibility. Head-only vs energy-tangent donor edits agree on correct donor recovery in 512/512,510/512,510/512 cases, and head-only obtains 38.09/43.75/48.44% correct donor targets compared with energy-tangent 38.09/43.75/48.05%. The median source donor norm fractions retained by head-only projection were ~45.7/46.9/47.0%; the global energy-tangent projection retained ~45.6/46.9/47.0%. Hence the additional scalar energy gradient removes approximately one coordinate but has limited observed behavioral importance here.

A 0.25× natural-donor-radius global energy-tangent arm achieves only 0.98/0.39/0.98% donor target correctness, consistent with a sharp finite-dose sensitivity. The globally tangent natural-radius edits show almost exactly zero **current-head** logit displacement (~9e−6 L2), yet the *finite nonlinear scalar energy* E increases on average by +18.36/+13.40/+21.01 total energy units (vs +12.11/+9.16/+13.56 for head-only edits). Thus first-order energy tangency does not yield finite E preservation at the test radius, and external E does not identify a unique causal gradient-flow mechanism.

The spatial support ablation supplies a stronger clue. Natural donor replacement at only the **predecessor+goal** token pair obtains 96.48/97.27/95.90% correct donor outcomes, close to full-state donor 98.24/98.24/98.44%, while goal-token-only donor obtains 66.02/63.67/63.09%. The natural two-token pair holds median 88.4/87.7/87.5% of total donor displacement norm across teachers. A local information-transfer path between the source predecessor site and its target is the obvious next native mechanism to isolate. This is compatible with the cyclic-copy task and does not establish such localization in other reasoning architectures.
