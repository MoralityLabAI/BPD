## Physical mechanism and energy caveat

The natural pair radius median was 8.85/8.80/8.83 Euclidean state units. The head-and-energy tangent retained median 39.2/41.9/41.7% of the source donor direction's norm prior to rescaling. Pair-head-only correct-target counts were 185/512,242/512,225/512, *higher* than the head+energy tangent counts 180/512,229/512,211/512. At natural radius, the tangent edit raised the frozen learned E on **every observed source-goal case**, with mean finite E increases +15.11/+12.42/+15.98 total energy units. Zero `dE(h)·z` is an **infinitesimal constraint**; it does not imply a finite Lyapunov decrease, nor does the scalar E identify an intrinsic optimization objective or actual model flow decomposition.

The gradient-matched oracle null raises donor correct-target success from the near-zero random K baseline to approximately 13–18% correct. Yet it remains 22–27 percentage points below source-donor-specific K edits. This demonstrates causal future-useful information in the **head-dark physical pair direction** beyond its *one signed donor-minus-original-class first-order margin*. It does **not** exclude a multi-logit first-order explanation of the ten-class argmax: the other eight competing digit logit sensitivities remain free in the strict signed-margin-matched controls. Second-order/Hessian terms, higher-order recurrent routing and finite off-manifold effects also remain possible.

Thus the first LGA-08 evidence sharpens the LOOPSCOPE line from simple energy descent to **source-specific delayed causal transfer through current readout-dark directions**, while the role of the frozen external scalar energy remains weak. It is a synthetic/frozen tiny TRM experiment and must not be described as proof of learned BPD neurons or language-model reasoning.
