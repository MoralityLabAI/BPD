# AMM-08: seven-action rank-three recurrent causal-memory transfer

**Prospective registration 2026-11-18, before AMM08 source or any training/development models.** Extends the AMM01–07 negative and instrument findings. AMM07 identified external judge reliability on 59/64 roots but gave only +.305 percentage points routing benefit; its 128 confirmation roots and prior protected cohorts remain sealed. AMM08 tests whether a richer learned recurrent substrate and *seven physical intervention choices* provide usable behavioral headroom for cross-model strategy memory.

## Disclosed engineering selection exposure

A local **engineering-only** prototype trained six-dimensional rank-three three-pass recurrent transitions from scratch at 400 Adam teacher updates / 240 BPD factor updates, with eight arbitrary excluded seeds 11,13,17,19,23,29,31,37 (outside all AMM08 phase namespaces), 256 cases each. It used seven nonempty subsets of three SVD-canonical factors, current-dark equal-norm patches and a two-future-readout success target. Across these eight exploratory models, mean best fixed-subset success was **42.19%**, mean per-case test-label oracle **54.69%**, a 12.5-point gap; full-donor intermediate replacement yielded 100% on this engineering set. These are **architecture-selection observations**, not prospective scientific evidence. Fixed registered choices below are frozen *before* AMM08 training/development.

## Genuine learned rank-three task and labels

Independent unit: **one independently trained physical 6D rank≤3 weight-tied recurrent operator and separately trained 6D rank≤3 BPD factor pair**. Physical teacher `F(h)=(h@I)@O` with `I[6,3], O[3,6]`, initialized from SHA256-derived root-specific torch streams, Adam LR=.035, **400 updates**, batch 96, sample source `(a,b,c)` uniform [-.8,.8] with `h0=(0,0,0,a,b,c)`. Root family wren/basalt by index parity: source readout orders wren (a,b,c), basalt (c,a,b). The target is `q(h1)=first, q(h2)=second, q(h3)=third`, where `q(h)=h[0]`. Minimize the sum of all three pass-specific target MSEs + .0002 mean squared physical W, with a single shared recurrent W on all passes.

Freeze physical teacher. Separately initialize `V[6,3], U[3,6]` and train BPD factors on root-separated source trajectories for **240 Adam updates** LR=.035, batch 96, minimizing the MSE of all three teacher intermediate states plus `0.5 * mean_square(VU - IO)`. Canonicalize the full reconstructed VU product with float64 rank-three SVD once, then use the canonical three components for all seven actions. Record independent source-task validation after fitting, source hashes, weight reconstruction residual and singular gaps. All roots, including failed fits, remain in every denominator.

## Causal donor intervention and seven physical actions

For every donor-recipient pair draw a recipient source triple uniformly in [-.3,.3]^3. Perturb all three source coordinates independently: first-readout nuisance coordinate by a random signed magnitude uniform [.15,.70], second- and third-readout target coordinates each by signed magnitudes uniform [.25,.45]. The target is **both source-defined donor second and third outputs** `(y2,y3)`, set from source coordinates independently of model weights/BPD factors *before fitting*. The donor's current first-pass readout is never an allowed policy target.

At recipient first-pass `r1=F_BPD(h_rec)`, compute full physical donor change `d1-r1=F_BPD(h_don)-F_BPD(h_rec)`, with per-case dose `D=||d1-r1||_2`. Candidate masks, ordered lexicographically, are **(0),(1),(2),(0,1),(0,2),(1,2),(0,1,2)**. For each mask `S`, construct the canonical donor component write `z_S=((h_d-h_r)@V[:,S])@U[S,:]`, set `z_S[0]=0` to project into the *current-readout kernel*, and normalize `||z_S||_2 = D` with fixed e1 fallback for degenerate projections. Deliver **one chosen mask** to the recipient after pass one, continue the **learned** tied BPD operator for passes two and three, and score an independent success iff **both** `|q(h2)-y2|<=.18` and `|q(h3)-y3|<=.18`. Preserve every failed pair, zero-dose case and root. Equal-dose isotropic random-dark A/B, zero patch sham and full donor intermediate replacement (distinct dose) are fixed controls. Native BPDModel/TiedComponentLinear parity must be demonstrated on excluded engineering roots at three passes.

## Allowed agent probes and frozen candidate features

Every decision agent receives *exactly one common unpatched continuation and one patched continuation per each of seven candidates*, observed at **both future readout horizons**. This costs **16 unlabeled readout measurements** per case (2 for unpatched, 14 for 7 patches). The full donor future continuation, external target y2/y3, all candidate success bits, and clean local calibration labels are **audit-only**. Agent action selection functions receive only these allowed features, their training memory and local corrupted calibration labels. Candidate feature vector length **18**:
1 intercept; 2 candidate mask cardinality / 3; 3 current recipient first-pass readout; 4 current donor minus recipient first-pass readout; 5 common physical dose D; 6 and 7 candidate p2/p3 readout shifts from unpatched; 8 and 9 their absolute values; 10 and 11 their squares; 12 product of both shifts; 13 and 14 candidate raw p2/p3 readouts; 15 cosine alignment of physical patch with full donor write; 16 normalized smallest SVD gap / largest singular value; 17 first-pass nuisance dose fraction `|d1[0]-r1[0]|/(D+1e-8)`; 18 family bit. Signs, mask order and tie resolution (lowest mask index) are fixed.

## Source-locked training memory and equal-budget local comparison

TRAIN 64 independent AMM08 roots with **128** independent donor cases/root; get *all seven physical candidate success labels* on each training case. Fit one global 18-dimensional ridge regression to binary candidate success (ridge penalty **10** on slopes, unpenalized intercept), equal sample weight per independent model root. Freeze exactly 18 coefficients rounded to four decimals (AMM07 numeric-replay correction), pooled best fixed-mask action, source hashes and candidate-bit root digest **before** any development root generation. Retain full raw float training data with separate source run artifact; behavioral lock compares source/bit identities and quantized policy.

Each DEVELOPMENT/CONFIRMATION root receives **16 new local calibration donor cases**, with all seven candidate success bits measured, each independently flipped with known probability **η=.20** using root-, candidate- and case-keyed deterministic judge streams. Each agent receives the identical **112 noisy local binary labels**, candidate features and 16 unlabeled readout measurements per future test case. Both have the same local action/query budget; memory additionally pays the disclosed amortized 64-root corpus.

Freeze the primary `memory_plus_local` policy: for each calibration candidate/case, compute the global prior clipped to [0,1], create unbiased corrected label target `(reported_y-.20)/.60`, and fit an 18-feature ridge residual with penalty **20** on every coefficient (intercept also penalized). Add residual to global prior; choose the highest predicted score per heldout case. Primary strong control `local_ridge`: fit the exact same 18 features and all 112 labels from scratch to unbiased corrected targets with ridge penalty **2** (unpenalized intercept); choose largest prediction. Secondary registered controls: `memory_only` (no local update), and `local_count` (highest empirical noisy-local candidate rate, with lowest-index tie-break). Other descriptive arms: `pooled` from training, per-case root-keyed `random_mask`, equal-dose `random_dark` and `random_dark_b`, zero `sham`, larger-dose `full_donor`. All arm choices and per-case success bits are retained.
