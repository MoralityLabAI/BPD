## Mechanism and intervention

Every independent model root learns a genuine **6D rank-three, three-pass, weight-tied physical transition** by gradient descent on three externally defined successive readout targets. Its separately initialized BPD V/U factorization is trained from teacher trajectories and independently canonicalized by a float64 full-product SVD. The two task families change which source coordinate is the first-pass nuisance and which provide the second/third donor target.

The donor perturbs all three inputs. One of **seven nonempty canonical component subsets** is projected to the immediate current-readout kernel, renormalized to the identical within-pair physical L2 dose, and injected after pass one. It must repair **both** independently defined donor readouts after two further passes. Actions are ordered `(0),(1),(2),(0,1),(0,2),(1,2),(0,1,2)`; index 6 is the full three-component subset. Equal-dose random-dark A/B, no-patch sham and larger-dose full-donor replacement are retained as controls.

Each decision rule sees exactly the same 18 candidate features/action, derived from the actual learned physical state and 16 unlabeled continuation measurements per case. The donor's future continuation and source-defined targets remain **audit-only**. Cross-model memory pays an explicit amortized 64-model training corpus cost. New model roots give every arm the same 16 local calibration donor pairs with all seven candidate intervention labels independently corrupted at p=.20, totaling **112 noisy local labels per root**. The proposed memory-plus-local ridge-residual rule and from-scratch local ridge compare on identical local labels, candidate features and physical dose.

### Crucial mechanism discrimination: the learned policy mostly collapses

The training-only memory selected the full 3-component mask on **32,605/32,768 cases (99.50%)**. Its other 163 choices were all mask 0 and occurred in basalt; they produced exactly the same total successes as the fixed pooled mask. The learned 18-feature readout therefore supplied **no net behavioral benefit over a single globally fixed intervention** on this development cohort.

The memory-plus-local policy used the full mask on **30,958/32,768 cases (94.48%)** and changed actions on 1,810 cases relative to memory-only. That local adaptation reduced correct outcomes by 64 cases. The from-scratch local ridge used full mask on only 18,238 cases and spread the remainder across six component subsets. Its weaker average performance means the significant primary result can largely be explained by a **strong pooled full-subset prior** and noisy local overfitting, rather than learned model-dependent causal routing.

The per-case audit-only oracle choosing from all seven true test outcomes scored **19,354/32,768 (59.06%)**, creating **6,355/32,768 = 19.39 points** of prospective task headroom beyond local ridge. That oracle accesses the test answer and is forbidden from policy inference. Its headroom indicates a richer task instrument, while the actual learned policy remains close to constant.

The registered noise channel flipped **1,424 of 7,168** calibration labels (19.87%), consistent with fixed p=.20.

## Follow-on implication

A future AMM-09 should preregister a *limited component-slot intervention budget*, such as choosing at most two of the three canonical components while matching each candidate's physical L2 dose, to prevent the full-subspace write from becoming an almost universal action. It should independently measure whether learned model- and case-conditioned routing beats a strong pooled budget-matched mask and a local ridge with the same noisy labels. The new task must have fresh train/development/confirmation root namespaces; the AMM08 cohort cannot be used for tuning or retrospectively establishing that hypothesis.

AMM-08 demonstrates a working, higher-rank learned causal-intervention instrument with genuine casewise oracle room, alongside a negative answer to the registered **meaningful cross-model memory advantage** question. This remains a **synthetic trained linear recurrent result**, with no direct inference about nonlinear transformers, pretrained TRM/LLM mechanisms, deployed cooperating agents or Buzz/Nostr memory exchange.
