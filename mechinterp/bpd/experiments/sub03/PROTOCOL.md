# BPD-SUB-03: prospective independent-root strong-null calibration

**Registration first, 2026-11-18.** This document is the first SUB-03 commit. It precedes implementation, all new trained SUB-03 models, and any calibration outcome. Follow-up to [SUB-02 PR #90](https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/BPD/pull/90) and [issue #94](https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/BPD/issues/94). **Phase is independent mechanistic-resolution calibration, not scientific confirmation.**

## Evidence and precise hypothesis

The frozen SUB-02 eight-root development run reported at rank eight a mean matched physical L2-dose improvement \`S_PCA-S_OBS=+0.4335874\` terminal-logit RMS and a 95% root-bootstrap interval [+0.3589334,+0.5346586]. Endpoint-specific observability explains that experiment; the nonzero 0.10815 RMS projected next-hidden commutator prevents equating it with a complete state-transition quotient. SUB-03 asks whether the *specific* future-output observability subspace carries more of the registered counterfactual effect than close, exactly same-rank, spectrum-preserving misalignments. Successful calibration is a prerequisite for additional confirmation planning, rather than proof of substrate-independent consciousness or any physical mind-substrate transfer.

## Exact-spectrum, equal-dose *predeclared* strongest null family

Eight sham projectors per root, constructed **only from discovery eigenvectors and frozen seeds** before any scoring.

1. **Four locally misaligned model-observability Gramians** at mixing angles theta={0.20,0.40,0.60,0.80} radians. Rotate the four weakest retained eigenvectors of the rank-eight Gramian toward its four discarded eigenvectors via a seeded independent 4x4 Haar orthogonal mixing, leaving its four strongest retained directions fixed. Write the 12x12 rotation as Q, define \`C_sham=Q C_obs Q^T\`, and use its top-eight subspace \`P_sham=Q P_obs Q^T\`. This preserves **all Gramian eigenvalues exactly**, rank and physical L2 dose while changing the candidate's alignment to the source dynamics. For each rank-8 local rotation record \`||(C_sham-Q C Q^T)||\` (construction parity), projector orthogonality, dose, and angular/eigenvalue-law checks.
2. **Four global spectrum-preserving Haar misalignments**: fixed independent seeded Haar \`Q\` in hidden coordinates and \`P_sham=Q P_obs Q^T\`. The implied \`C_sham=Q C_obs Q^T\` keeps identical eigenvalues and rank, with unrelated physical orientation. These probe a broader misalignment family while the local rotations supply the strongest relevant null.

Using the **512 split-3 calibration states**, calculate \`D_null,j = S(P_pca)-S(P_sham,j)\` for all eight shams, plus the source \`S(P_pca)\` and \`S(P_obs)\`. For a directional claim that the *specific* Gramian subspace produces a positive causal advantage, define a one-sided **root-level false-positive null envelope** \`N_i=max(0,D_null,1,...,D_null,8)\`. The full signed per-sham values remain archived, including negative gains. Calibrate the 95% quantile at 95% confidence using the maximum order statistic of **at least 59 eligible independent root-level N_i**, not the 8x64 within-root observations. A failed root blocks the 59-root gate; report its outcome and preserve denominator 64. The calibration explicitly tests fine-grained direction specificity relative to nearby rotated Gramian alternatives and is stronger than simple random-orientation shams.
