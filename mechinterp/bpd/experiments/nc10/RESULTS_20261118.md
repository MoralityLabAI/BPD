## Population, external tasks and model integrity

The frozen NC-10 study trains **eight independent *pairs*** of fully learned 20D four-pass recurrent models, or 16 independent network initializations. Each pair's A and B networks are trained separately for **384 Adam steps**, each with its own 2048-case training stream. No weights, optimizer state or hidden activations are shared during training.

Tasks are related by one externally defined branch: when `x4>0`, both label `sign(x0)`; otherwise task A labels `sign(x1)` and task B labels `sign(x2)`. Model-only discovery uses a separate 1024-case stream, source-only alignment uses up to 1024 paired unlabeled inputs (positive context only), and true-label evaluation uses a disjoint 1024-case stream per pair. The statistical independent unit is **one pair of trained models** (n=8), not 16 models, task contexts, tokens, recurrent passes, sham draws or intervention variants.

All **8/8 pairs** passed the full technical admission gates: each model's task/context competency and dense-weight threshold, native target-vs-full BPD parity, source/target rank-four eigengaps, at least 300 paired positive-context alignment examples, the eight discovery-only physical-dose-matched shams, the target-model exact orthogonal factor-gauge control and zero-output-head negative control. Across roots, clean A and B model context accuracies averaged approximately **95–96%**.

## Frozen four-contrast decision

The source projector is the top-four per-pass eigenspace of A's **own final-logit gradient**. A separate B projector is fit to B's own frozen model on identical unlabeled discovery inputs. Fit orthogonal `R_t` from *paired positive-context pre-`mix` activations* only, and transport `P_{A→B,t}=R_t^T P_{A,t} R_t`. Then use the native BPD override-read full closed-loop intervention with the same four shared physical `mix` calls.

The primary transfer outcome compares **externally labeled B task NLL** of the aligned A projector against the B-owned rank-four projector. ## Causal and geometric diagnostic detail

The *mean held-out B task NLL*, pooled by pair root and scored on identical B labels, shows how transport fares:

| Physical B-`mix` intervention | Shared context NLL | Task-private context NLL |
|---|---:|---:|
| Full clean B model | **0.092203** | **0.118925** |
| B-owned rank-four projector | 0.110656 | 0.128278 |
| **A→B paired Procrustes projector** | **0.288062** | **0.891504** |
| A projector in unaligned/identity chart | 0.742870 | 0.740574 |
| A projector from *permuted* positive-context alignment | 0.532778 | 0.884703 |

Paired Procrustes improves shared-context NLL by **+0.454808 nats** against the raw unaligned projector, a predeclared descriptive control. It does not robustly beat the *permuted correspondence* after independent-root correction, and its shared-branch performance remains worse than the B-owned rank-four intervention. Mean A→B transported projector overlap with B's own projector is **0.40337** as `tr(P_A→B P_B)/4`. This is descriptive and has no independently calibrated random-overlap/null hypothesis.

The **per-pass Procrustes fit** has substantial residual: the largest within-root relative alignment residual averaged **0.759**, ranging from 0.654 to 0.858 across the eight roots. This reflects distinct learned input charts in two separately trained recurrent models. It is qualitatively different from the **exact within-B factor rotation** test, where the maximum physical projector-map relative discrepancy was below **6.17×10^-8**, and maximum kept-logit discrepancy below **1.73×10^-6**. The gauge test verifies intervention code; the Procrustes fit measures an approximate empirical cross-model association.

## Scientific limitations and next hypothesis

The positive task-specificity result is expected from the designed task relation, and alone does not establish a portable causal subspace. A rank-four projector can preserve model B's own task in B's learned physical coordinates while an aligned same-rank projector from separately trained model A fails. The distinction between **shared input-driven prediction** and **shared causally sufficient physical write subspace** survives this negative result.

A future cross-model study would need a separately registered, explicit transport objective that learns *causal-response correspondence* from **unlabeled or separately authorized development interventions**, perhaps matching local physical write Jacobians or task-conditioned observable quotients. Such an alignment has a different sample/access budget from NC-10's ordinary activation Procrustes and would require fresh untouched training/evaluation roots and baseline controls. The current source may serve as a fixed negative reference.

These eight paired roots do not calibrate the nonparametric 95th-percentile sham variation with 95% confidence (requires at least 59 independent sham roots for the sample-maximum rule), nor justify a confirmation release. The separate prospective confirmation population and protected ARG-03-N, AMM-03 and other sealed experiments remain **untouched**.
