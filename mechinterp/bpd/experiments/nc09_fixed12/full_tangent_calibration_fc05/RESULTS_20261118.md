## Why this calibration was run

FC04 found eight matched shams on all 16 of its new development models. Its mean sham-minus-DP12 effect was 0.0096263567 nats. That cohort exposed the sampler's behavior and lacked the retained discovery tensors needed for a full independent reconstruction of its source moments.

FC05 prospectively assigned 59 fresh roots to calibration and retained the source arrays, chosen projectors, model states, numerical traces and final task logits. Each trained model is the independent statistical unit. The eight shams and 1,024 task cases within a model provide repeated measurements. The roster contains no replacement roots.

The model and training recipe stayed at 20 dimensions, four tied nonlinear passes and 384 Adam updates. Supervised training used external labels. Selection used 1,024 discovery cases and model-self derivatives. Heldout task labels were opened after source selection and the independent source audit. All task contrasts below use the original final NLL receipts, whose values were independently checked from the retained logits.

## What the two sham summaries measure

For root i, the registered rootwise statistic is the largest absolute deviation of a sham's NLL from that root's median sham NLL. Its maximum over the 59 roots is **0.0581835732 nats**, attained at root 16052.

Under independent, identically distributed model roots drawn using the fixed selection law, the sample maximum bounds the population 95th percentile of this rootwise statistic with confidence at least **1 − 0.95^59 = 0.9515054748**. This confidence is exact for a continuous distribution and conservative when it has atoms. Complete retention of the registered roots is part of that argument. The bound concerns variation among constructed sham orientations within a model. Applying it to a different sampler or root population requires a new justification.

The separate FC02-derived calculation estimates a planning threshold for the mean of 32 pseudo-target contrasts. For each root, a uniformly chosen sham serves as a pseudo-target against the other seven. That conditional contrast has mean zero by construction. The unchanged nested bootstrap used 256 outer whole-root resamples and 4,096 inner simulated means, with seed 18180018. Its threshold, **B_mean32 = 0.0041955868 nats**, yields the registered hurdle **B_mean32 + 0.020 = 0.0241955868 nats**.

This bootstrap provides approximate planning evidence for a different statistic. A scientific use would require a validated coverage argument for the proposed aggregate test and justification for comparing the DP12 intervention with the constructed sham distribution. The simulation counts leave the number of independent calibration models at 59.
