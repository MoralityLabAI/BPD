## Why matching induced nonrandom calibration missingness

NC09-FC02 frozen same-tuple local projector proposals at principal angles 0.20, 0.30, 0.45 or 0.65 radians were screened against three source-only restrictions: **physical retained write RMS within 15%**, signed clean first-order final-model-score mean within max(.005,25% of its original magnitude), and first-order score RMS within max(.005,25% of original RMS).

Among **40 fully admitted** roots, the median absolute source score mean was **0.1600** and median score RMS **0.3766**. Among **19 failed-admission** roots these medians were **0.0699** and **0.1963**. Smaller score effects led to narrower permitted absolute score deviations, reducing available same-angle local sham orientations. This is a measured association, not a controlled demonstration of the sole cause.

The subset admitted under these stronger null controls is selective: its mean fixed-arm DP12 advantage over flat3 was **+0.024038 nats**, versus **+0.009403** on the nineteen excluded roots. A root-mean null calibrated on only forty admissible models would change the intended model-root source distribution. FC02 correctly declined to compute its prospective aggregate threshold, bootstrap gates or power decision.
