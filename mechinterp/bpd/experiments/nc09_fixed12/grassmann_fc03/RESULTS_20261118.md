## Mathematical diagnosis and next independent design

A fixed-angle chart enforces nontrivial displacement from DP12, while an eight-dimensional complementary-frame coordinate search must satisfy three nonlinear moment constraints. The dimension count suggests room for feasible solutions in generic tangent geometry; particular recurrent models can have very small source-score moments, source-degenerate constraint Jacobians or disconnected feasible regions that this restricted chart misses.

A later distinct preregistration could:
- compute the three FC02 source metric gradients with respect to **full Grassmann tangent coordinates**, rather than eight preselected complementary-frame coefficients;
- project candidate directions into the approximate null space of the metric Jacobian while controlling nonlinear second-order residuals, then retract onto the rank-preserving manifold at a frozen nontrivial angle;
- evaluate feasibility and **same-rank source-matched causal null effects** on new independent model roots, with original FC03 roots treated as exposed engineering data, and separately validate a root-mean sham-null calibration before scientific release.

That is a mathematical proposal, not an unregistered rescue of FC03.
