## Mathematical and physical source audit

Each selected DP12 subspace was retained at **fixed principal angle θ=.25 radians**, giving normalized DP12 distance \`sin(.25)=0.2474039593\`; the frozen minimum was .20. The minimum independently decoded pairwise sham distance across the original 128 projectors was **0.297767**, above the frozen .18 lower bound.

All source-selected shams passed the original FC02 three matching inequalities: retained physical write RMS error≤.15, absolute signed first-order model-score deviation≤max(.005,.25|m_DP12|), and model-score RMS deviation≤max(.005,.25 v_DP12). The largest recorded relative physical write RMS deviation was **0.050063** across the selected projectors, well within the .15 tolerance; signed/RMS score moment violations were individually checked against their model-root-specific source tolerances during the original source-only execution and again against the archived residual records.

Every accepted projector's float64 **4×20×20 little-endian** matrix and SHA256 was archived with the first-run model-root JSON; the original CI independently decoded those bytes and checked rank, symmetry, idempotence and both separation conditions.

The full chart generally has roughly **200 complementary frame parameters**, compared with FC03's eight. For the common (4,3,3,2) tuple, the full chart has **202 coordinates**. In this original experiment **all 348 recorded constraint-Jacobian evaluations had numerical row rank three** at the prospective singular-value threshold 10^-8. The 128 accepted projectors used **348 total damped Jacobian corrections**, a mean of 2.719 per sham (median two), with maximum 14 and five accepted at initial iteration zero.

**Audit limitation:** The original root artifacts contain complete projector bytes, per-root source target moments and matching residuals, with source hashes and native task receipts. They do **not** include all 1024 clean discovery \`z,g,a\` tensors. The post-result independent archive auditor can replay projector geometry and recorded FC02 inequality bounds, while full independent recomputation of the W/m/v moments from original source tensors would require their separate retention in a new run. The original source-locked executable evaluated the full source moments at every accepted candidate.

This is evidence compatible with much prior apparent dark-fiber specificity being explained by local task-score sensitivity. It does not itself quantify a causal mediation share.

## What this does and does not unlock

FC03 failed eight-sham admission on three of sixteen model roots. FC04's larger tangent source chart and three-metric correction found valid nonduplicate controls for every one of sixteen fresh models. That supports **a new prospective 59-independent-model-root calibration using this exact stronger null**, after an independent-source power/variation protocol is frozen.

FC04 does **not** establish a significant 32-root causal effect, a distribution-free root-mean null or an absolute mechanism identification. The original FC01 **CALIBRATION_REDESIGN_OR_PILOT_ONLY**, FC02 **CALIBRATION_INCOMPLETE** and FC03 **ENGINEERING_NULL_OR_INELIGIBLE** remain unchanged, as do protected confirmation populations. No new 59-root sham recalibration and **zero 32-root scientific confirmation models** were generated here.

The next scientific decision should require the task-derivative-matched sham null to clear an independently calibrated root-mean effect threshold with the original same-rank and discovery-dose controls, and an exact EvalMath power/variation preflight. It must preserve this original FC04 development cohort as source-exposed engineering, not reuse its 16 roots as new calibration.
