## Actual terminal task performance

| --- | ---: | ---: |

| **Fixed-budget 12-slot source-DP pass allocation** | **12** | **0.116478** |
| Flat rank3, gain 1 | 12 | 0.143658 |

The source-DP method retained **25% fewer physical write dimensions than flat rank4**, with an additional mean true-label NLL cost +0.007012 and upper root bootstrap +0.012430. Compared with the equally sized flat-rank3 source, it had a +0.027179-nat task benefit. **All 16 independent roots** individually favored DP12 versus both unscaled flat3 and source-RMS-dose-matched flat3, though some individual wins were small.

The dose-matched gain alpha was computed before test labels as clean derivative-discovery physical-write RMS(DP12) divided by RMS(flat3). The realized source gain had mean **0.999956** and range **[0.972897,1.041753]**, with exact retained write RMS equality at source precision. The two independent paired primary comparisons therefore support an advantage attributable to pass-specific allocation rather than gross retained physical write dose. Edited-state future-write RMS and task Jacobian dose are not exactly matched by this source control.

Average retained ranks by pass were **(4.1875, 3.25, 2.5625, 2.0)**. Every original root assigned greater capacity to early recurrent passes, with only two retained directions at the final pass. The fixed reversed-pass-rank placebo, which retains the same four ranks and total budget but changes their temporal positions, had **mean true-label NLL 0.224750**, or **+0.108272 nats** worse than DP12. All 16 roots favored the source-selected temporal order over reversal.

This last result was preregistered as **descriptive**, and reverse-ordered projectors can have different clean retained-write dose and nonlinear downstream effects. It supports further investigation of temporal causal allocation rather than a separate confirmed hypothesis.

The registered mean/whole-root gates nonetheless passed. Preserve that counterexample and its complete raw receipt; no per-root universal-fidelity or per-root sham dominance claim follows.

This controls native orthogonal factor gauge, fixed physical rank and clean-source retained write RMS. It does not match the exact future-loop state distribution, true-label directional gradient, downstream Jacobian response or nonorthogonal factor gauge.

## Why the result changes the research direction

NC-07's model-only adaptive rank achieved task fidelity on many roots but failed the registered 8/8 physical sham gate and mean-rank compression criterion. NC-08's source-only smallest-budget selector achieved 32.8% physical rank compression and full sham admission but exceeded its extra true-label NLL upper limit versus flat rank4. The **independent fresh-root** fixed-budget NC-09 experiment removes the exposed adaptive budget-selection step and directly tests the NC-08 secondary causal allocation hypothesis.

With the capacity and dose both controlled, a passwise allocation of the same physical write rank had a consistent behavioral advantage across the sixteen newly trained recurrent models. This is a stronger synthetic causal-subspace result than covariance/eigenvector alignment, source Gaussian-mass identification or teacher-self predictive KL alone, because all comparisons used independently labeled **future task outcomes under actual closed-loop physical interventions**.

The inference is limited to this task, architecture and 16 independent model roots. The observed positive development gate remains **engineering evidence** rather than scientific confirmation. A new preregistration should predeclare the source-DP fixed12 model and its matched flat3, dose-flat3 and same-tuple sham controls, obtain independently powered 59-root sham calibration and an EvalMath model-root variation floor before touching an independent >=32-root scientific confirmation population.

The unused separate NC-09 cross-task-transfer branch, original NC-04–08/PD-15–17 historical first-run results and protected ARG-03-N/AMM-03/BPD-CQ-08 sources remain unchanged.
