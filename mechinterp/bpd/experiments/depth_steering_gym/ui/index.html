What the repair changes

Full-coordinate causal Jacobian

DT03 differentiates every cell and class against the full physical state, rather than summing normalized class probabilities. The native H/L continuation retains the receiver's declared input.

Outcome and physical control

Whole-case recovery, delivered intervention dose and full signed-task-Jacobian controls form the future measurement contract. Trained-model causal evidence has yet to be collected.
