## Design consequences, without first-run relabeling

A new prospective follow-up could make the **compression target explicit** before optimization, with rank cap four, source-only abstention, and an independently powered task-fidelity endpoint. It should separately register sufficient discovery-only physical-sham supply and account for potential high-rank ineligibility. The current 1,024-proposal matched-sham pool cannot be enlarged retroactively for NC-07. New source/model seeds would be required, and any ranks/risk thresholds chosen from NC-07 are exposed development choices.

A second mathematical direction is to estimate task-conditioned retained physical-write error on legal source edits and compare with actual future-loop task derivatives. That would build on NC-05/06 gauge-covariant physical projectors, Compactification's protected tangent rank, and LOOPSCOPE's observation that recurrent energy descent does not guarantee useful terminal steering.

NC-07 remains **eight-root engineering-development evidence**.
