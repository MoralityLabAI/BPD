## Mechanistic reading and next test family

The result extends NC-11's target-information asymmetry into an even smaller calibration regime. The source A projector contains substantial score-gradient information from 1,024 A examples, and its q-specific alignment uses B gradients plus a 128-row B activation prior. The equal-B-gradient direct B estimator still yields lower true-label NLL, even when q is just eight examples. Source A's own rank-four projector remained locally faithful to its own model (mean final Bernoulli KL(clean A||P_A) **0.0058004 nats**). Its limitations arise in **cross-model physical correspondence**.

The deficit demonstrates that preserving one model's decision-output gradient geometry through an orthogonal chart does not establish preservation of a distinct learned recurrent model's physically causally sufficient write subspace. The target model's direct gradients already point toward its own useful directions, and downstream nonlinear recurrence can amplify small chart errors. All comparisons use matched physical write RMS but cannot equalize downstream causal derivatives or future-state distribution.

**Evidence remains synthetic engineering on eight independent pairs.** Rootwise percentile bootstrap is fragile with n=8 and one task family. The nonparametric 95th-percentile sham variation at 95% confidence still requires ≥59 independent sham roots for a maximum-order-statistic bound. Confirmation is unregistered and sealed; a new independent root population and EvalMath mechanistic-power preflight are required before a general claim.

A logically distinct follow-up would test **cross-model causal-response operator matching** or an alignment learned to preserve *interventional output contrasts* rather than raw score-Jacobian rows. Such an estimator needs a prospectively fixed source intervention/donor budget, target information-accounting table, task-label seal, and a separately defined null. Do not refit NC-12's 0.05 ridge, q family or shams using its original held-out outcome data.
