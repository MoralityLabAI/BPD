## Registered decision

The primary fixed rank was **8**, comparing the SUB-01 all-pass state-PCA projection with a discovery-only future-output Jacobian Gramian \`E[J^TJ]\`. In both arms, identical raw Gaussian impulses were projected to the four-dimensional discarded fiber and normalized to the same post-pass-one physical L2 dose, \`alpha=.25×RMS(||h1||)\`. Remaining passes executed on newly altered recurrent states.

- All 8/8 independent roots 1300–1307 passed source task competence (minimum observed per-context accuracy 97.2%), Gramian geometry checks, finite-difference derivative checks, exact transported gauge closure and physical dose/null-projection checks.
- All 8/8 roots had a positive rank-8 \`S(PCA) - S(Gramian)\` terminal-logit RMS effect, with mean **+0.4335874**, above registered engineering minimum +0.15 logit RMS. The preregistered 2000-draw whole-root bootstrap (seed 130202) returned 95% interval **[+0.3589334,+0.5346586]**. Registered outcome: **ENGINEERING_SIGNAL_ONLY**.
- Rank-8 PCA mean terminal-logit response was **0.4698572** RMS; Gramian mean was **0.0362697**, about **92.3%** lower sensitivity. Mean clean output Bernoulli KL from teacher was 0.012437 nats for PCA and 0.007828 nats for Gramian. The latter remains an endpoint-specific construction and is not an exact quotient of the full hidden-state dynamics.
- Rank-8 projected next-hidden state commutator norms (RMS Euclidean) were 0.24762 for PCA and 0.10815 for observability. The remaining nonzero Gramian commutator is relevant to interpretations of genuine recurrent mechanism equivalence.
- The secondary, preregistered rank-10 comparison gave average PCA response 0.412625, Gramian response 0.018134, and gain +0.394491 logit RMS. These rank-10 values are **descriptive**, excluded from the registered rank-8 decision.
