## The source-only selector has a task-fidelity mismatch

Across eight independent roots, mean clean heldout external-label NLL was 0.106053 nats. Mean selected NLL was **0.128766**, versus **0.119258** for flat rank-four and **0.130279** for flat rank-three. The selected source-only KL mean at its chosen budget was approximately **0.01664 nats**, below the registered 0.027 selector cap for all roots.

Root **800** demonstrates the limitation: a source-only clean-to-retained final KL of **0.015908** selected budget ten with ranks (3,3,2,2), while the independent true-label task cost was **+0.059849** and the extra task NLL versus flat rank-four was **+0.051944**. Teacher-prediction agreement by KL is a model-only diagnostic and need not tightly constrain true-label task loss or the stronger-rank comparator for every independent trained model.

The absolute heldout NLL fidelity criterion was a mean and whole-root upper confidence bound, so root 800's individual cost exceeding 0.05 did not invalidate that **separate** registered gate. It did contribute substantially to the failed selected-versus-flat4 upper interval.

## Physical audit and boundaries

Every root independently trained for 384 Adam updates with source split sizes 2048 train, 1024 self-score derivative discovery, 512 model-only rank validation and 1024 independently labeled true-task evaluation. The physical 20D dense recurrent mix was applied on four tied nonlinear steps. Full-target BPDModel/TiedComponentLinear closure passed in 8/8, with zero full-mask output discrepancy in the original recorded closure. The maximum native-versus-orthogonally-rotated selected projector write operator discrepancy was below **1.24×10^-7** relative, and maximal selected full-loop logit difference was below **1.85×10^-6**.

The passwise source proxy estimates squared omitted first-order model-score response. Its sum across recurrent steps neglects downstream Jacobians and nonlinear cross-step interactions, which the independent model-only closed-loop KL selector partly checks. A low model-self KL remains an imperfect guard for external-label NLL. The physical matched shams control rank tuple and clean discovery retained-write RMS, while the edited future-state distribution and terminal Jacobian remain imperfectly matched.

The original study satisfies strong capacity-reduction, absolute task fidelity and sham-specificity development checks, with a **failed** additional task-cost bound versus flat rank-four. No trained recurrent task circuit was scientifically confirmed. The independent 59-root sham-calibration, EvalMath power/variation preflight and 32-root confirmation populations remain sealed, and no original NC-04 through NC-07, PD-15–17 or protected BPD-CQ-08 results were modified.

A fresh follow-up could preregister a **fixed budget-12 source-DP allocation** and compare it directly against flat rank-three and flat rank-four under independent new models, using the same physical sham/closed-loop fidelity controls. That would separate the allocation advantage from the conservative-or-aggressive model-self KL budget selector without relabeling the NC-08 first-run result.
