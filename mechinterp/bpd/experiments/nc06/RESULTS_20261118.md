All had native full-mask BPDModel/TiedComponentLinear closure, rank-three eigengaps, eight matched local rank-three physical dose shams for each method, and correct orthogonal basis transport. ## Why this differs from PD15–17

PD-15's sparse identity source had a strong norm-cardinality signal and norm weighting reduced effective sample size to about 48%. PD-16 proved weight-to-observation pairing essential, and PD-17 removed the norm/cardinality signal under radial camouflage while preserving independently observable latent axes.

In NC-06's actually learned recurrent activations, the normalized norm weighting was much weaker:

This shows that the tilted discovery measure closely resembles the original on this trained source, consistent with the small observed causal differences. It does not prove that ESS reduction alone determines task mediation.

All eight roots met the first-run physical sham admission requirement. The minimum number of source-only dose-qualified candidates out of 1024 proposals was 36 for norm-r3, 24 for uniform-r3 and 40 for shuffle-r3, all above the eight required shams.

## Scope and preserved holds

This is a development-only experiment on independently trained nonlinear four-pass tied models with an external task. It establishes native physical causal specificity of a rank-three gradient subspace in this fixed synthetic model family. It does **not** establish added task utility from norm-conditioned covariance, nor confirm a recurrent mechanism in pretrained large models. A separate fresh-root study would require a new prospective weighting law, model-root power planning and at least 59 sham-calibration roots before 32-root confirmation.

**Zero sham-calibration and zero confirmation roots were evaluated** by this run. Original NC-04/05, PD-15/16/17 and protected unrelated source populations remain unchanged.
