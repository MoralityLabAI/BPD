# BPD-NC-01: recurrent neuron communities (prospective protocol)

**Status:** source/design registration; no trained NC-01 model, discovery sample, or NC-01 held-out response has been inspected at this commit.  
**Reference:** Shilov et al., *Discovering Neuron Communities in Language Models*, Goodfire, 17 Nov 2026, https://www.goodfire.com/research/neuron-communities.  
**Scope:** a *new* synthetic, weight-tied recurrent student/teacher task. This is a method transfer, **not** a reproduction of Goodfire's Llama results or a claim of recovered semantic circuits.

## Hypothesis and estimands

A target logit margin backpropagated to physical write sites identifies pass-specific groups of neurons/components whose causal influence is concentrated across more than one recurrent pass. Compare:

1. **NATIVE:** post-activation neurons, exactly represented as component reads with `V=I`, `U=W^T`.
2. **BPD-SVD:** the full-rank, exactly reconstructing SVD initialization of BPD's tied factor pair, **not a learned BPD dictionary**.
3. **ROTATED:** seeded orthonormal read basis, `V=Q`, `U=Q^T W^T`, an exact reparameterization.

A learned BPD dictionary and SAE basis are reserved follow-up arms: their residual channels and reconstruction losses must be separately accounted for before selective-sufficiency claims.

Primary root-level effects are
`necessity = E[NLL(ablated)-NLL(clean)]` and
`selective_sufficiency = E[KL(clean || retained)]`.
The first asks if the intervention harms teacher-label prediction; the second asks if the retained coordinates preserve the clean model's output. Compare to matched random masks on the **same** clean model and same frozen held-out examples. Also report agreement, write displacement RMS and per-pass/last-pass KL. Whole-target closure takes precedence over every arm score.

## Registration, sampling and seals

* **Independent unit:** teacher + independently trained student weight/root seed. Multiple prompts, recurrent passes, target tokens, and masks within one root are repeated observations. Any root-level bootstrap must resample entire roots.
* **Engineering only:** seeds 100–103; 1024 disjoint generated discovery examples and 1024 disjoint evaluation examples per root, selected deterministically before scoring. A CI smoke may use seed 100 and fewer examples and is explicitly non-inferential.
* **Prospective confirmation:** a fresh set of at least 64 independent roots, to be locked in a later source-only manifest **only after** an EvalMath mechanistic-power preflight on development root effects and matched-sham variation. No confirmation seeds or samples are generated by the NC-01 initial CI. Keep all exploratory tuning inside the engineering population.
* **Training:** weight-tied 4-pass small recurrent MLP teacher and student; teacher trajectories supply intermediate distillation targets. BPD factorization is performed after student training. Freeze student/teacher before any discovery/causal evaluation. Data are independent random Gaussian inputs; this tests a recurrent mechanism on a known teacher, not mathematical reasoning.
* **Attribution:** sign-aligned final logit margin; per-pass `activation * gradient_of_margin` in the read-coordinate basis, retaining positive contributions. Select the largest eight positive coordinates across all passes per sample.
* **Graph:** per-activation-node co-selection adjacency, frequency-adjusted phi coefficient, require >=12 co-occurrences, phi >=0.15, mutual top-4 neighbors; connected components of 2–12 nodes qualify. Nodes are `(pass, coordinate)`, with an explicit pass-aggregated summary. All constants are fixed for this version; no rescue tuning if zero communities qualify.
* **Selection:** rank qualifying components by positive total internal graph edge weight on the discovery sample, deterministic tie-break by node IDs; score at most the top three discoveries per basis per root. Compare only the top discovery across bases for any cross-basis claim.
* **Matched shams:** 8 seeded random groups per discovery, preserving the number of nodes **within each pass**. Generate candidates on discovery data, report their induced write RMS relative to the target; apply a preregistered 15% allowable dose mismatch and mark the null comparison invalid if fewer than 8 shams qualify. Never select controls using held-out outcomes. Distinct shams are required.
* **Parity gate:** full-ones mask vs native target at all four readouts: max absolute logit difference <=5e-5 and KL <=1e-7 nats (float32). The all-zero intervention and any keep-only arm have an explicit zero residual-factor mask. If full weight residual norm exceeds 1e-4 relative to target, block intervention claims.
* **Intervention:** pass-indexed masks of `TiedComponentLinear` on a **complete closed-loop** rollout; no clean-window teacher-forcing. Necessity disables selected coordinates; sufficiency retains only selected coordinates in affected physical linear, including its shared use at other passes. A per-pass individual intervention is a separate secondary assay.
* **Held-out endpoints:** final correct-label NLL, clean/arm KL for all pass readouts, teacher-label accuracy and pass-level write-displacement RMS. A high cross-pass attribution graph is observational until an eligible matched-sham causal effect follows.

## Hypothesis gates (confirmation only)

For a positive mechanistic claim, require all of:

1. One eligible community spanning >=2 passes in >=75% of independent roots.
2. Root-mean necessity improvement over the median dose-matched sham >=0.02 nats, with root-bootstrapped two-sided 95% CI entirely >0.
3. Root-mean selective sufficiency advantage against the median matched sham >=0.02 nats, with root-bootstrapped two-sided 95% CI entirely >0. This means `KL_sham_keep - KL_community_keep` is positive.
4. Unadjusted mean necessity >=0.05 nats and retained final KL <=0.05 nats, applied rootwise before pooling.
5. The engineering-root EvalMath preflight supports the declared effect floor and multiplicity family (3 bases; one primary community per root). If underpowered, report `NOT RESOLVED`; retain the predetermined confirmation seal.
6. Component results and rotation results use matched selection exposure, complete model reconstruction, physical intervention-dose diagnostics and the same eligible-root population.

A failed parity gate, insufficient qualified shams, or insufficient statistical resolution is a **failed/blocked experiment**, not a positive or negative mechanistic discovery. Report all screened roots and exclusion counts. For the engineering population, report raw effect sizes and diagnostics without inferential hypothesis labels.
