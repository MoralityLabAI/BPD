## Main causal lesson

The stationary-to-correct-clock mean reduction was **0.0245299 state RMS**. Giving the wrong-clock families their own fitted source optimization recovered **0.0227793 RMS**, or **92.9%** of that reduction. The residual +0.0017506 RMS is approximately **7.1%** of the original stationary-to-aligned gain. In the *same eight SUB-06 models*, the gap from the correctly fitted clock to the best post-fit time permutation was +0.0219133 RMS; the gap to the best independent source-fitted wrong clock was +0.0017506, a roughly **92% reduction in apparent clock-specific advantage**.

The source-only underlying *true-clock* Frobenius closure loss A averaged **0.028641** for the aligned source fit and **0.033338** for wrong schedule 5. This close physical-source fit is consistent with temporal directions remaining partly interchangeable under the present small synthetic task. ## Interpretation and next research question

SUB-05 established a repeatable engineering advantage over post-fit time permutations. SUB-06 shows that nearly all of that advantage can also be obtained by giving wrong-clock bundles their own source-fitting training budget. The new, stricter registered **temporal-source-alignment** hypothesis failed its meaningful-effect gate. Correct-clock fitting retains a small measurable effect at the sampled dose, with no scientific confirmation.

The present source task repeats the same four-feature input at all four passes and admits high-performance short-depth approximations. A future experiment needs to test whether **genuinely order-dependent computation** generates stronger pass-specific causal geometry. Prospective work should use distinct time-indexed inputs or a source-native iterative task with explicit shallow-computation controls, independently trained roots and matched source-fitted wrong-clock nulls. The training competence and depth-necessity gates must be frozen before any such roots are trained.

The result concerns small synthetic neural recurrences and finite-domain causal representations. It provides no physical evidence for biological substrate independence, actual mind uploading, or preservation of phenomenal experience.
