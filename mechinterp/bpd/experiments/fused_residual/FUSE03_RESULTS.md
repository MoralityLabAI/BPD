## Bounds and screen
Let each scalar/tensor element have exact-real magnitude at most A and arithmetic error at most E. For two such inputs (A,E),(B,G), a product has magnitude AB and error at most AG+BE+EG+u(A+E)(B+G)+eta. A sum/subtraction has magnitude A+B and error at most E+G+u(A+B+E+G)+eta. A length-k dot has magnitude kAB and error bounded in the standard round-to-nearest model by k(AG+BE+EG)+gamma_(2k) k(A+E)(B+G)+2k eta/(1-2ku). Here u=2^-24, eta=2^-126, gamma_(2k)=2ku/(1-2ku). These conditional absolute bounds use global tensor maxima and conservative float64 scalar inflation. They assume supported arithmetic, finite inputs, no overflow and bounded underflow as specified; actual BLAS implementation not formally verified. Compute the envelopes of the original and reassociated linear output graphs and sum their errors, taking max with the routing clean-output bound. Mask differences use |m-d|<=1 for masks in [0,1]. Refuse sphere paths for envelope mode. With bound E on difference and candidate y, a sufficient elementwise mixed-tolerance test is E<=atol+rtol*max(0,min|y|-E), assuming the bound holds. No backward certificate. The scalar envelope is deliberately coarse and cannot establish general floating-point safety. The guard did not admit any stress cases.

Heuristic screen: before candidate GEMMs require max|V|,max|U|<=2 and max|x|<=16; after GEMMs require finite candidate, no row satisfying max(|dY|+|ZU|+|mean|)>64*(max|candidate|+.001), and sphere threshold margin 5% of eps when applicable. Test final output finite. No caching stats, no pruning learnable masks. Guarded experimental subclass invokes original reference once if rejected, with original ambient autograd. Target mode, calibration-update calls, unsupported CPU precision/device/autocast use reference. No production integration. This screen is not a theorem, and misses gradient failures.

## FUSE-04 recommendation
Freeze FUSE03 as a negative training guard result. Prefer preserving the original residual matrix arithmetic by reusing a differentiable reconstruction inside a fixed-weight rollout. With T calls, remove (T-1)D_in*C*D_out MACs in residual reconstruction, retaining the same per-invocation F.linear and masking equations. Cache scope must be one parameter-version and one autograd lifecycle; no detach in training, no reuse after optimizer update or separate backward; test no-grad-to-grad boundary, aliasing/parameter mutation and calibration semantics. Separate eval-only frozen cache and graph-scoped training cache. Gradients may differ by accumulation order, so verify severe cancellation and ordinary optimizer tests independently. Measure actual native throughput and overhead; do not promote without stress and end-to-end checks.
