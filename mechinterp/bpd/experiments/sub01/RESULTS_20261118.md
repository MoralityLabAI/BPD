## Interpretation and limitations

This is a direct synthetic illustration of the gap between a **good clean-output approximation** and an **intervention-preserving quotient**. PCA captures high-variance clean states, which need not coincide with the directions having low sensitivity under off-manifold perturbation. The tested impulses deliberately push h1 along an exact kernel of the projection with norm 25% of the discovery h1 norm. They may leave the clean reachable-state manifold. Thus the experiment refutes *the registered quotient under the specified intervention domain/dose*, rather than proving that every low-variance coordinate is necessary during natural inputs.

The exact gauge control is a coordinate conjugacy and establishes measurement parity. It does not establish equivalent consciousness, continuity of a subject, or identical physics across different material substrates. Eight independently trained models are a development cohort, with no 59-root independent sham-tail bound or EvalMath-ready confirmation. Follow-up analyses of these same eight roots are descriptive and may not change the preregistered null.

## Candidate next experiment: new roots and causal geometry

A newly preregistered study should compare PCA source-state covariance against **model-only future-logit gradient covariance** in independent new trained roots. For an impulse after pass one, the first-order sensitivity of terminal logits is

\`g_1 = J_2^T J_3^T J_4^T v\`,

where the recurrent Jacobians are evaluated on the clean trajectory. A projection preserving the dominant eigendirections of \`E[g_1g_1^T]\` directly targets the observed hidden-to-output sensitivity, while PCA targets \`E[h h^T]\`. Test matched ranks 8 and 10 with exactly norm-matched null-space impulses at two prospectively registered doses (e.g. 0.05 and 0.25 of discovery h1 RMS norm), plus next-state commutator, clean KL and independent root-level sham controls. This would distinguish linear local sensitivity from nonlinear off-manifold effects. The next protocol must precede generating any new models or reviewing any new data.
